# Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party (MVMCP) Knowledge Base

## Event Overview

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party is a special ticketed event held on select nights at Magic Kingdom during the holiday season. It features exclusive entertainment, character greetings, holiday overlays on attractions, and complimentary cookies & cocoa throughout the park.

**Key insight**: Time goes MUCH quicker than you'd imagine at these parties. The event is expensive, so having a strategy helps you get your money's worth. You cannot do everything - you must choose between prioritizing characters OR rides, while hitting the main entertainment events.

### What's Included
- Exclusive access to Magic Kingdom (7pm - 12am, entry starting at 4pm)
- Special parade, fireworks, and stage shows
- Holiday overlays on select attractions
- Complimentary cookies, cocoa, cider, and eggnog at stations throughout the park
- Rare character meet opportunities
- Dance parties
- Holiday theming and atmosphere (snow on Main Street!)

## Priority Framework

Most party-goers prioritize in this order:

### Priority #1: Main Entertainment Events
- Mickey's Once Upon a Christmastime Parade
- Minnie's Wonderful Christmastime Fireworks
- Mickey's Most Merriest Celebration Stage Show
- Frozen Holiday Surprise

### Priority #2: Special Overlay Attractions
Rides with unique holiday theming only available during the party season:
- **Space Mountain - Holiday Run!!** ⭐ (FAVORITE - it's a blast!)
- **Jingle Cruise** (available during regular park hours too - do it then if possible)
- **Tomorrowland Speedway** ⭐ (like driving through Christmas lights!)
- **Mad Tea Party** (cute but not worth going out of way for)
- **Monsters Inc. Laugh Floor** (cute but not worth going out of way for)

### Priority #3: Cookies and Cocoa
- Multiple stations around the park
- Different cookie at each location
- Choice of cocoa, cider, and/or eggnog
- Lines longer first few hours, usually walk-through by ~10pm
- Kids love collecting cookies; adults often find them processed (eat the special party treats instead!)

### Priority #4: Character Greetings
- TONS of rare characters only available at the party
- WARNING: Can have 2-3 HOUR waits for popular characters (like Sandy Claws & Sally from Nightmare Before Christmas)
- Will eat up bulk of your party time if this is your focus
- Check MDE app "Characters" dropdown after 7pm for all locations/times

## Attraction Strategies

### General Wait Time Patterns
- Lines surge at beginning of night
- Lines tend to DROP as night goes on
- **Shortest waits**: Final hour of the party (11pm-12am)
- **During fireworks** (10pm): Waits drop in half almost instantly
- **During parades** (8:30pm, 11pm): Also good times for shorter waits

### Realistic Expectations
Even with strategy, expect 25-30+ minute waits for headliners during the party. If you're used to short waits from Lightning Lanes, this may be frustrating.

**Team's typical party**: 3-5 attractions + 2 short character greetings + all main events (7pm-12am)

### Attractions by Category

**Holiday Overlay Attractions** (party exclusive or seasonal):
| Attraction | Notes |
|------------|-------|
| Space Mountain - Holiday Run!! | ⭐ BEST holiday overlay - absolutely do this! |
| Jingle Cruise | Fun but time-consuming. Available regular park hours too - do it then instead |
| Tomorrowland Speedway | ⭐ Love this - like driving through Christmas lights! |
| Mad Tea Party | Cute overlay, not worth prioritizing |
| Monsters Inc. Laugh Floor | Cute overlay, not worth prioritizing |

**Best Nighttime Vibe Attractions**:
- Big Thunder Mountain - THE best ride after dark!
- Tron - Stunning at night
- Dumbo, Speedway, Magic Carpets, PeopleMover, Astro Orbiter - All fun after dark

**Headliners to Take Advantage Of** (shorter waits than regular days, no LL needed):
- Tron
- Tiana's Bayou Adventure
- Seven Dwarfs Mine Train
- Peter Pan's Flight

### Attraction Strategy Tips

1. **Save headliners for final hour** (11pm-12am) for shortest waits
2. **Jump in line at fireworks start** (10pm) - waits drop dramatically
3. **Be choosy** - can't do tons of rides AND all events
4. **If attractions are priority**: See 10:35pm stage show, then use final hour for rides
5. **Last minute strategy**: Hit shortest waits first, then get in line for longest wait (Tron or SDMT) right before midnight

### For Toddlers/Young Kids
- Best to do Fantasyland & kid attractions **before** party starts (4pm-7pm)
- These lines are super short during party too, but can interfere with entertainment
- Dance parties are great - no waiting in line!

## Character Greetings

### The Reality
- Some of the LONGEST lines of the party
- Wait times vary drastically (some short, some 2-3 hours)
- Sandy Claws & Sally from Nightmare Before Christmas = longest waits
- Will eat up most of your party time if this is your focus

### Strategy
1. **Choose priority 2-3 characters max**
2. **Get in line as early as possible** (some start at 6pm, most at 7pm)
3. **Check MDE app** "Characters" dropdown AFTER 7pm for all locations/times
4. **Save one greeting for end of night** or during parades when waits drop
5. **Accept the trade-off**: Lots of characters = fewer rides and possibly missing events

### Critical Truth
**It's impossible to**: Do all main events + meet lots of characters + do lots of attractions

**Pick your lane**: Main events + characters OR Main events + rides

## The Parade

### Why It's Special
The parade is THE highlight of the party. The snow falling on Main Street is magical and the floats are delightful. Many party veterans watch BOTH parades!

### Parade Route & Timing
- **Starts**: Frontierland
- **Ends**: Main Street USA (front of park)
- Takes ~15 min to reach Castle
- Takes ~20 min to reach back of Main Street

**Warning**: If you stake out a spot 30 min early, you may wait nearly an hour for parade to reach you!

### Best Viewing Spots

**Main Street USA** (RECOMMENDED):
- Only place with SNOW falling!
- Closer to front of park = lighter crowds (people don't want to walk far)
- Arrive at parade START time - still ~20 min before it reaches you
- Arrive earlier for front-row spot

**Hall of Presidents (Liberty Square)**:
- Good last-minute spot
- Usually roomy, further back but better viewpoint

**Right side of hub, near Tomorrowland bridge**:
- Last-minute option
- Further back but often has room

**Sneaky Spot**: Behind Ye Olde Christmas Shoppe (Liberty Square), by bridge to Adventureland
- Usually NO ONE here
- Profile view of floats from distance
- Great if you don't need to be close

### First vs Second Parade
- **First parade (8:30pm)**: More crowded
- **Second parade (11pm)**: Lighter crowds, recommended for late-nighters

## The Fireworks

### Minnie's Wonderful Christmastime Fireworks
- **One showing only**: 10pm
- Draws a BIG crowd - everyone wants to see special Christmas fireworks!

### Best Viewing Spots

**Main Street USA** (for projections + fireworks):
- Closer to hub = great but fills up to 1 HOUR early
- Middle/back of Main Street = can get spot ~20 min in advance

**Tomorrowland Bridge**:
- Trees block part of castle
- Still see all fireworks
- Can grab spot 5-10 min in advance
- Great if planning to hit Tron or Space Mountain right after

### Pro Tips
- Actual wait for many rides drops IN HALF at fireworks start
- If you're near Tomorrowland, make a beeline for Space Mountain or Tron right at 10pm for super short wait
- After fireworks, Space Mountain and Tron get a SURGE in line - avoid unless you were already watching nearby

## Stage Show Strategy

### Mickey's Most Merriest Celebration
- Festive and super cute
- Shows at: 7:40pm, 9:25pm, 10:35pm, 11:55pm
- **Lighter crowds**: Last two showings (10:35pm, 11:55pm)
- Sometimes almost NO crowd by final showing

### Smart Strategy
**Watch 10:35pm show → Stay in spot → 11pm parade arrives**
- You wait ~15-20 min for parade to start
- "Kill two birds with one stone"
- Parade comes to YOU

## Eating Strategy

### The Challenge
Figuring out when to eat is the most stressful part of these events!

### Recommendation
**Eat a full meal BEFORE 7pm** (around 6pm is ideal)

Why:
- Much easier to enter party with full stomachs
- Food is chaotic at Magic Kingdom from 7pm-9pm
- You can eat lighter during party since you'll be munching cookies & cocoa

### During the Party
- Fun party snacks, pretzels, or small meals when convenient
- Don't try to have a big sit-down meal during prime party hours

## Sample Itineraries

### Itinerary #1: Early Birds

**Best for**: Families with younger kids, seniors, anyone who won't make it to midnight, or those wanting attractions/characters in final 90 minutes

**Strategy**: See main events FIRST, then attractions/characters when waits drop

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 4pm - 7pm | Attractions, character greetings, dance parties |
| 7pm - 8pm | Continue above OR line up for priority character |
| **7:40pm** | **Mickey's Most Merriest Celebration Stage Show** |
| 8pm - 8:30pm | Quick break, cookies/cocoa, one attraction, OR line up for parade spot |
| **8:30pm** | **First Parade** (~8:50pm when it reaches Main Street) |
| 9:15pm - 9:45pm | Attraction in Tomorrowland or Club Tinsel, then grab fireworks spot ~15-20 min early |
| **10pm** | **Fireworks** |
| 10:15pm | Can exit OR continue... |
| 10:15pm - 12am | Leftover character greetings or attractions. Save Tron/SDMT for final hour for shortest waits |

**Notes for Early Birds**:
- Get to stage show 10-15 min early (earlier for close spot)
- After parade, small window before fireworks - use for attraction, cookies/cocoa, snack, or restroom
- Tomorrowland bridge = great fireworks spot if hitting Tron/Space right after
- After fireworks, Space Mountain/Tron get SURGE - avoid unless you were watching nearby

---

### Itinerary #2: Late-Nighters

**Best for**: Night owls staying until midnight, those wanting lighter crowds at events

**Strategy**: Use early hours for priorities, see second parade and later stage shows

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 4pm - 8:15pm | Character greetings, attractions, dance parties based on your priorities |
| 8:15pm - 9:45pm | Must-do attraction (Space Mountain, Tron, or character greeting if <45 min). Or dance parties, cookies/cocoa. Grab fireworks spot 15-30 min early |
| **10pm** | **Fireworks** |
| 10:15pm - 11pm | Attraction(s) or food break. OR stay for 10:35pm Stage Show |
| **11pm** | **Second Parade** (lighter crowds!) |
| 11:35pm - 11:55pm | Quick attraction, snack, or dance party |
| **11:55pm** | **Final Stage Show** (lightest crowds!) |
| After midnight | Main Street shops usually open another hour! Great for shopping and photos |

**Alternative Late-Night Ending**:
- See 10:35pm Stage Show
- Watch 11pm Parade
- Use final minutes to ride: One walk-on (Mad Tea Party, Winnie the Pooh, Buzz) + One headliner (Tron or SDMT) right before midnight

**Notes for Late-Nighters**:
- Second parade (11pm) has lighter crowds than first
- Final stage show (11:55pm) sometimes almost empty
- Watching final show maxes out your time since rest of park closes
- After final parade, waits for Tron/SDMT are often very short!

## Pro Tips

### General Tips
1. **Get the event map** when you enter - essential even for pros
2. **Eat before 7pm** - food is chaotic during party hours
3. **Check MDE app** for character times/locations after 7pm
4. **Snow only falls on Main Street** - watch parade there!
5. **Second parade and later stage shows** have lighter crowds
6. **Final hour** (11pm-12am) = shortest attraction waits
7. **Fireworks start** = waits drop in half instantly

### Time Management
- Event goes by MUCH faster than you think
- Our team fits: 3-5 attractions + 2 short character greetings + all main events
- You CANNOT do everything - pick characters OR rides
- Character lines can be 2-3 hours for popular ones

### Must-Do Attractions
1. **Space Mountain - Holiday Run!!** - Best overlay, absolutely worth it
2. **Tomorrowland Speedway** - Like driving through Christmas lights
3. **Big Thunder Mountain** - Best ride at night (not holiday exclusive but amazing)

### Skip During Party (Do on Regular MK Day Instead)
- Jingle Cruise (available during regular park hours)
- Standard Fantasyland dark rides (unless you have extra time)

### Attraction Timing
- **Jump in line at 10pm** (fireworks) for dramatically shorter waits
- **Final hour** (11pm-12am) for headliners = shortest waits
- **Right after fireworks** = SURGE for Tomorrowland rides (avoid unless nearby)
- **During parades** (8:30pm, 11pm) = shorter waits

### Character Strategy
- Get in line early (6pm or 7pm when they start)
- Limit to 2-3 priority characters
- Save one for end of night or during parade
- Accept you'll miss some attractions if characters are your focus

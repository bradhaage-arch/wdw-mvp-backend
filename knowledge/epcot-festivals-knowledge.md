## EPCOT INTERNATIONAL FOOD & WINE FESTIVAL 2025

### About This Checklist
The official WUAP 2025 Checklist includes every food item that scored over a **7.5/10 review** from their (picky) team. This includes **30 items** (food only, not drinks).

### Important Note on Festival Food
Festival food is historically **ridiculously inconsistent**, down to the minute. Typically this depends on:
- How fresh (or not fresh) items are
- The ingredients available that day
- The Cast Member that prepares them

**Bottom line:** If you see something you want to try, just try it. The worst-case scenario is you don't like it.

## FOOD & WINE STRATEGY TIPS

### Maximize Your Experience
1. **Start at World Showcase opening (11am)** - Fewer crowds at booths
2. **Bring a cooling towel** - Festival is during hot season
3. **Share items** - Portions are small, great for sampling many things
4. **Prioritize Ali's favorites** - If short on time, hit the ⭐ items first
5. **Check the festivals app** - Some booths have shorter lines at different times

### Budget Tips
- Festival food adds up quickly ($6-12 per item typically)
- Plan your must-haves vs. nice-to-haves
- Consider doing a Festival day separate from a "full park" day

### Best Time to Visit Booths
- **Early (11am-12pm):** Shortest lines, freshest food
- **Mid-afternoon (2-4pm):** Longer lines, some items may run out
- **Evening (after 6pm):** Atmosphere is best, but lines can be long

## OTHER EPCOT FESTIVALS (Quick Reference)

### Festival of the Arts (January-February)
- Focus: Art, music, and culinary arts
- Highlights: Food studios with artistic presentations, Disney on Broadway concert series

### Flower & Garden Festival (March-July)
- Focus: Gardens, outdoor entertainment, fresh flavors
- Highlights: Outdoor kitchens, topiaries, Garden Rocks concert series

### Food & Wine Festival (Late August-November)
- Focus: Global cuisines and beverages
- Highlights: Global marketplaces, Eat to the Beat concert series, cooking demonstrations

### Festival of the Holidays (November-December)
- Focus: Holiday traditions around the world
- Highlights: Holiday kitchens, Candlelight Processional, cookie stroll

## KEY STRATEGIES CAPTURED

### Lightning Lane
- **Refresh Hack = #1 strategy**
- Book Hollywood Studios FIRST (Slinky sells out)
- Book Animal Kingdom LAST (easiest)
- Tiers only apply during prebooking
- End-of-night: actual wait = 30-50% of posted

### Dining
- 24-hour manual refresh beats apps 80%+ of time
- OpenTable for Disney Springs = way better availability
- Lounge dining hack: Tambu Lounge = 'Ohana food, no reservation

### Park Strategies
- **MK:** Start Tomorrowland/Fantasyland, work counterclockwise
- **EPCOT:** World Showcase opens 11am, morning for rides
- **HS:** Rise OR Slinky at Early Entry, then opposite land
- **AK:** Evening waits drop drastically after 4-5pm

### Resort Rankings (Top 5)
1. Polynesian (25 pts)
2. BoardWalk (24 pts)
3. Yacht/Beach Club (24 pts)
4. Animal Kingdom Lodge (23 pts)
5. Riviera (23 pts)

### Adult Trip Highlights
- **Monorail Crawl:** Trader Sam's → Tambu → Enchanted Rose → Steakhouse 71
- **Skyliner Crawl:** La Cava → AbracadaBar → Bar Riva → Banana Cabana
- **Lounge Hack:** Tambu Lounge serves 'Ohana menu without reservation!

### Toddler Trip Essentials
- Rent stroller from outside company (not Disney's)
- Mid-day break is NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Baby Care Centers in every park
- Rider Switch for parents to experience thrill rides

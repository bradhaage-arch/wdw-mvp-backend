# Hollywood Studios Knowledge Base

## Park Overview

Hollywood Studios is the **smallest park** but has **mostly headliner attractions** - meaning almost every ride has long wait times. This makes it unique among the four parks.

**Key insight**: This is THE park to budget for Lightning Lane Multi Pass. Without it, you WILL wait in 45+ minute lines for multiple rides. With LL, this park becomes much more manageable and can even be a "sleep in" day since you walk in with pre-booked selections.

### Why Hollywood Studios is Different
- At other parks, you can arrive early and knock out many rides with short waits
- At Hollywood Studios, by 9:45am (if park opens at 9), most rides are already 45+ minutes
- The rides are incredible but they ALL attract long lines
- Lightning Lanes make the biggest difference here compared to any other park

### Park Layout
- **Hollywood Boulevard** - Park entrance, shopping
- **Sunset Boulevard** - Tower of Terror, Rock 'n' Roller Coaster, Fantasmic! amphitheater
- **Echo Lake** - Indiana Jones Stunt Spectacular, Frozen Sing-Along, Star Tours, 50's Prime Time Cafe
- **Grand Avenue** - Muppet*Vision 3D, BaseLine Tap House
- **Toy Story Land** - Slinky Dog Dash, Toy Story Mania, Alien Swirling Saucers
- **Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge** - Rise of the Resistance, Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run, Oga's Cantina
- **Animation Courtyard** - Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway, Disney Junior, Voyage of the Little Mermaid

**Note**: Despite Galaxy's Edge being in the back, the park is compact outside of that area. Backtracking isn't as painful here as at other parks.

## Attractions

### The Big Two Headliners

| Attraction | Height | Lightning Lane | Strategy |
|------------|--------|----------------|----------|
| **Slinky Dog Dash** | 38" | Multi Pass (Tier 1) | Pre-book as Tier 1, OR Early Entry (arrive 30+ min before EE), OR end of night |
| **Rise of the Resistance** | 40" | Single Pass only ($) | Purchase LLSP (evening return time), OR end-of-night standby (actual wait often 50% of posted!), OR risky Early Entry (ride is down ~35% of mornings) |

**Slinky Dog Dash Reality Check:**
- 90-minute waits are AVERAGE most of the day, most times of year
- 2-3 hour waits during busy seasons
- It's a 2-minute coaster - amazing but nobody wants that wait
- This is why Lightning Lane matters so much at this park

**Rise of the Resistance Strategy:**
- Notorious for being DOWN in the morning (~35% of the time at opening)
- Planning Early Entry for Rise is risky - have a backup plan
- End-of-night waits are dramatically shorter than posted (60 min posted = ~18 min actual)
- If budget allows, LLSP is worth it for peace of mind
- Best LLSP return time: evening (reduces backtracking)

## Rope Drop & Arrival Strategy

### Transport Options
- Bus, Skyliner, ferry, walking (from EPCOT-area resorts), or driving

### Arrival Time Recommendations

| Priority | Arrival Before EE |
|----------|-------------------|
| Slinky Dog Dash or Rise of the Resistance | 45 minutes before Early Entry |
| All other rides | Right before Early Entry starts |

*Times are for medium crowds. Slower time = can arrive later. Busy time = double the recommendations.*

### Getting Into the Park
- Hollywood Studios can get congested - allow **15-20 minutes** for security + entry
- Even more during busy season

### Transport Timing

**Skyliner:**
- Starts ~1 hour before Early Entry (sometimes 45 min)
- Quick 5-minute ride from Caribbean Beach hub to HS
- All resorts except CB transfer at the hub
- Can also take Skyliner from Beach Club (fun alternative!)

**Buses:**
- Start ~1 hour before Early Entry
- If targeting Rise or Slinky, aim for one of the first buses

**EPCOT-Area Resorts:**
- **Swan/Dolphin**: Walking path ~10-15 min, OR ferry (faster if boat is there)
- **Yacht/Beach Club**: Ferry recommended. Walking is 1+ mile. Can also take Skyliner from Beach Club
- **BoardWalk**: Walk via path behind resort (fastest exit through pool area, NOT toward Atlantic Dance Hall end)

**Driving:**
- Parking opens 1 hour before Early Entry
- Allow ~20 minutes from parking gate to inside park

## Touring Strategies

### The Challenge
Hollywood Studios is a **puzzle** - harder to plan than other parks because:
- Lightning Lane availability varies wildly
- Rides go down frequently (especially Rise, Tower of Terror)
- Your plan will be dictated by what LLs you can book
- Tower of Terror sometimes runs only 2 of 4 elevators, affecting wait times

### With Lightning Lane Multi Pass (Recommended)

**Pre-Book Strategy:**
- **Tier 1**: Slinky Dog Dash (if unavailable: Rock 'n' Roller Coaster or Mickey & Minnie's)
- **Tier 2**: Tower of Terror AND Toy Story Mania
- **Single Pass**: Rise of the Resistance (evening return time)

**Day-Of Strategy:**
1. Scan into first LL as early as possible
2. Immediately book next priority (Slinky or Rock 'n' Roller Coaster - whichever you didn't get)
3. Keep booking - you can hold up to 3 at a time
4. Even if return times are pushed to afternoon, BOOK THEM
5. Try to modify/refresh for earlier times
6. Do shows, characters, and exploring between LL return times
7. Save Galaxy's Edge for evening (or flip the itinerary if you're Star Wars superfans)

**Key Insight**: Your touring order will be based on LL availability, not a rigid plan. Go with the flow!

### Without Lightning Lane Multi Pass

You WILL wait in long lines. Accept it and strategize:

1. **Early Entry is mandatory** - arrive 45 min before EE
2. During EE: Slinky Dog Dash first
3. At park opening: Rock 'n' Roller Coaster → Tower of Terror
4. Check Toy Story Mania wait - if ≤20 min, do it + Alien Swirling Saucers
5. By mid-morning, lines are LONG - shift to shows and entertainment
6. Star Tours ~3pm (waits usually more manageable)
7. Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway ~3:45pm
8. Evening: Galaxy's Edge touring
9. Smugglers Run last hour before close
10. **End-of-night Rise of the Resistance** - waits are hyperinflated (60 min posted often = 18-32 min actual)
11. Fantasmic! (can sometimes fit both Fantasmic and Rise depending on timing)

## Pocket Plans (Non-Morning Arrivals)

### Late Morning Arrival
No specific pocket plan because your first Lightning Lanes determine where to start.

**Strategy:**
1. Book Slinky Dog Dash LL for when you arrive (if possible)
2. If not available: Rock 'n' Roller Coaster or Mickey & Minnie's
3. Continue booking LLs for rides
4. Fill time with shows, characters, and Galaxy's Edge exploring

### Afternoon Arrival
1. Use first Lightning Lane, continue booking nearby attractions
2. Check show times - Frozen Sing-Along, Beauty and the Beast, Indiana Jones
3. Meet Olaf, see Vacation Fun (sit-down break)
4. Star Tours (if standby reasonable)
5. Villains show and/or Little Mermaid

7. Explore Galaxy's Edge

### Evening Arrival (All Ages)
1. Star Tours
2. Explore Galaxy's Edge, Rise via LLSP if purchased
3. Slinky Dog Dash*
4. Alien Swirling Saucers
5. Toy Story Mania*
6. Rock 'n' Roller Coaster*
7. Tower of Terror*
8. Fantasmic! OR Mickey & Minnie's/Rise end-of-night strategy

### Evening Arrival (Young Kids)
1. Frozen Sing-Along Celebration
2. Meet Olaf
3. Slinky Dog Dash*
4. Alien Swirling Saucers
5. Toy Story Mania*
6. Experience Galaxy's Edge
7. Star Tours (if desired, 40")
8. Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway*

## Young Kids Strategy

### Best Attractions for Young Kids
- Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway (no height requirement!)
- Toy Story Mania (no height requirement)
- Alien Swirling Saucers (32" - gentle)
- Slinky Dog Dash (38" - if tall enough, it's mild)
- All shows (Frozen Sing-Along, Little Mermaid, Beauty & the Beast, Disney Junior)
- Vacation Fun (Mickey Shorts film)
- Character meets

### Height Requirements to Note
- Alien Swirling Saucers: 32"
- Slinky Dog Dash: 38"
- Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run: 38"
- Star Tours: 40"
- Tower of Terror: 40"
- Rise of the Resistance: 40"
- Rock 'n' Roller Coaster: 48"

### Sample Young Kids Day (With LL)

1. **Pre-trip**: Consider LLSP for Rise if older kids/adults want to ride. Pre-book Slinky Dog as Tier 1
2. **Early Entry**: Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway OR head to Toy Story Land
3. Toy Story Land: Slinky Dog Dash (LL), Toy Story Mania, Alien Swirling Saucers
4. Book next LL immediately - prioritize Slinky or Runaway Railway (whichever you didn't get)
5. Breakfast/snack at Woody's Lunch Box
6. Check character greeting times (Toy Story characters, others around park)
7. Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway (LL) + Meet Mickey & Minnie at Red Carpet Dreams
8. **LL considerations**:
   - Adults want Tower of Terror/Rock 'n' Roller? Book now for rider swap
   - Packed time of year? Use LL for shows
   - Otherwise, check Olaf wait and meet him
9. Lunch: Backlot Express or ABC Commissary (or table service)
10. **Frozen Sing-Along** + Meet Olaf (check times for order)
11. Star Tours (40") via LL or standby
12. Afternoon shows: Little Mermaid, Beauty and the Beast, Vacation Fun
13. Book any remaining LLs
14. Evening: Galaxy's Edge - Blue & Green Milks, Kat Saka's Kettle (kid-friendly snacks)
15. **Dinner**: Backlot Express recommended (Docking Bay 7 doesn't have great kid options)
16. Fantasmic!

### Sample Young Kids Day (No LL)

1. **Early Entry**: Slinky Dog Dash (38")
2. If time in EE: Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway
3. Back to Toy Story Land: Toy Story Mania
4. Alien Swirling Saucers
5. Check character times, note important ones
6. Meet Mickey & Minnie at Red Carpet Dreams
7. Star Tours (40") if desired
8. Early lunch: Backlot Express or ABC Commissary (Sci-Fi is great for all ages!)
9. Frozen Sing-Along + Meet Olaf
10. Afternoon shows: Little Mermaid, Beauty and the Beast, Vacation Fun
11. Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway ~3:45pm if didn't do earlier
12. Early evening: Galaxy's Edge touring, kid-friendly snacks
13. **Dinner**: Backlot Express (better kid options than Galaxy's Edge)
14. If didn't ride Slinky: Get in line ~90 min before Fantasmic
15. Fantasmic!

## Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge

### Attractions
- **Rise of the Resistance** (40") - See headliner strategy above
- **Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run** (38") - Interactive, you "pilot" the Falcon. Has Single Rider line for shorter waits

### Experiences
- **Oga's Cantina** - HIGHLY recommend, but expensive. Usually needs reservation. Fun alien bar atmosphere
- **Droid Depot** - Build your own droid (~$100+). Can often walk up
- **Savi's Workshop** - Build your own lightsaber (~$250). Reservations strongly recommended, very popular
- **Blue & Green Milk** - Must-try unique drinks
- **Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities** - Amazing shop, legacy lightsabers
- **Numerous themed shops and stalls** - Explore everything!

### Dining
- **Docking Bay 7** - Quick service, Star Wars theming, good for adults/foodies
- **Ronto Roasters** - Quick wraps and breakfast items
- **Oga's Cantina** - Drinks and small plates
- **Kat Saka's Kettle** - Sweet/savory popcorn, kid-friendly
- **Milk Stand** - Blue and Green Milk

### Touring Strategy
Many people save Galaxy's Edge for evening - the land is stunning at night, crowds often thin out, and you can do Rise end-of-night strategy.

**Alternative**: If your party are huge Star Wars fans, flip the itinerary! Start in Galaxy's Edge in the morning, do Toy Story Land in the evening.

## Fantasmic!

### What It Is
Spectacular nighttime show in the amphitheater on Sunset Boulevard. Water, fire, projections, characters, boats - it's incredible and a MUST DO.

### Show Length
~25-30 minutes

### Arrival Strategy

| Crowd Level | Arrive Before Show |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Slow time of year | Walk up a few minutes before |
| Moderate crowds | 20-30 minutes early |
| Busy (Christmas, Spring Break) | 60 minutes early |

### Pro Tips
- If there are TWO show times, the second show is always lighter and easier to get into
- Can sometimes do both Fantasmic AND Rise of the Resistance end-of-night (depends on show time and Rise wait)
- Fantasmic Dining Package available - guarantees seating but requires table service meal

## Pro Tips

### General Tips
1. **Budget for Lightning Lane Multi Pass** - This is THE park where it matters most
2. **This can be a "sleep in" day** - With LLs pre-booked, you don't need to rope drop
3. **Rise of the Resistance is risky at rope drop** - Down ~35% of mornings
4. **End-of-night Rise waits are hyperinflated** - Posted 60 min often = 18-32 min actual
5. **Shows in the afternoon** - Don't waste end-of-night hours on shows when ride waits drop
6. **Park is small** - Backtracking isn't as painful here
7. **LL availability dictates your plan** - Be flexible, go with the flow
8. **Fantasmic is a MUST** - Plan your evening around it

### For Young Kids
- Toy Story Land is the sweet spot (Slinky, Toy Story Mania, Alien Saucers, characters)
- Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway is perfect (no height requirement, all ages love it)
- Shows are excellent and fill time between attractions
- Galaxy's Edge is cool to explore even without riding Rise
- Docking Bay 7 isn't great for kids - use Backlot Express instead

### Money Savers
- Skip Rise LLSP and use end-of-night strategy instead (risky but often works)
- Second Fantasmic show is easier to get into (no need to arrive super early)
- BaseLine Tap House has great snacks without table service prices

### Hidden Gems
- **BaseLine Tap House** - Best pretzels at Disney World, great beers and cocktails
- **Sunset Boulevard shops** - Some of the best shopping in all of WDW, often not crowded
- **Walt Disney Presents** - Free exhibit with cool Disney history and props
- **Hollywood Brown Derby Lounge** - Walk-up only, great for drinks without reservation
- **Second Fantasmic show** - Much lighter crowds

## Sample Itineraries

### Full Day with Lightning Lane (Adults/Older Kids)

1. **Pre-trip**: Purchase Rise LLSP (evening return), pre-book Slinky Dog (Tier 1), Tower of Terror + Toy Story Mania (Tier 2)
2. **Early Entry** (optional with LLs): Rise of the Resistance (if it's up - risky) OR Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway OR Smugglers Run/Toy Story Mania
3. Start in Toy Story Land or Sunset Boulevard (use first LL for Slinky, Tower, or Rock 'n' Roller)
4. **Your touring is now dictated by LL availability** - book continuously, refresh for earlier times
5. When you can book second Tier 1: Slinky Dog or Rock 'n' Roller Coaster (whichever you didn't do)
6. Breakfast/snack at Woody's Lunch Box or Joffrey's donuts
7. Book Smugglers Run LL for evening when you have a free selection
8. Mid-morning: Shows + entertainment between LL returns (Frozen Sing-Along, Beauty & the Beast, Indiana Jones - pick 1-2)
9. Walt Disney Presents exhibit, Little Mermaid show
10. Lunch: ABC Commissary (or Rosie's/Catalina Eddie's/Fairfax Fare if near Tower)
11. Tower of Terror + Rock 'n' Roller Coaster via LLs (try to book close together since they're next door)
12. Villains stage show, Sunset Blvd shopping
13. Star Tours via LL or standby
14. BaseLine Tap House for pretzels and drinks
15. Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway (LL) and/or Star Tours if haven't done
16. **Evening: Galaxy's Edge** - Oga's Cantina (reservation), Blue & Green Milks, shopping, Droid or Lightsaber building
17. Dinner: Docking Bay 7
18. Smugglers Run via LL (or Single Rider for short wait)
19. Rise of the Resistance via LLSP (or end-of-night standby)
20. **Fantasmic!** (can sometimes fit both Rise and Fantasmic)

### Full Day WITHOUT Lightning Lane (Adults/Older Kids)

1. **Pre-trip**: Consider Rise LLSP if budget allows (evening return)
2. **Arrive 45 min before Early Entry**
3. **Early Entry**: Slinky Dog Dash (38")
4. At park opening: Rock 'n' Roller Coaster (48") → Tower of Terror (40")
5. Check Toy Story Mania wait - if ≤20 min, do it + Alien Swirling Saucers
6. **Lines are now long** - shift to shows and exploration
7. Walt Disney Presents, Little Mermaid show
8. Lunch: ABC Commissary (or sit-down at 50's Prime Time or Sci-Fi)
9. Afternoon shows: Frozen Sing-Along, Beauty & the Beast, Indiana Jones (pick 1-2)
10. Villains show, Sunset Blvd shopping
11. BaseLine Tap House for pretzels and drinks
12. Star Tours ~3pm
13. Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway ~3:45pm
14. **Galaxy's Edge evening touring**: Oga's Cantina, Blue & Green Milks, shopping, experiences
15. Dinner: Docking Bay 7 or Woody's Lunch Box
16. Toy Story Land ~6pm if missed any rides
17. Smugglers Run last hour before close
18. **Fantasmic!** (plan around show time)
19. **Rise of the Resistance end-of-night** (actual wait often 50% of posted!) - can sometimes fit both Rise and Fantasmic

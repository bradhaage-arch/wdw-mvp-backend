# Lightning Lane Knowledge Base

## Overview

The Lightning Lane system consists of two main products that allow you to skip standby lines at Walt Disney World:

### Lightning Lane Multi-Pass (LLMP)
- Includes 40+ attractions across all four parks
- Pre-book up to 3 selections per day
- Book additional selections after scanning into each LL
- Prices: ~$15-$50/day depending on park and crowd level

### Lightning Lane Single Pass (LLSP)
- Premium "pay to play" for 5 headliner attractions (1 per park, 2 at Magic Kingdom)
- NOT included in Multi-Pass - sold separately
- Pre-book up to 2 per day
- Prices: ~$15-$30 per person per ride

### Lightning Lane Premier Pass
- Premium service with all LL access in one park
- Limited availability, very expensive
- If using this, you don't need strategy - just use it!

## Tier System (Multi-Pass)

### How Tiers Work
During **prebooking only**, Disney applies tiers to keep Lightning Lane distribution "fair":
- **Tier 1**: Choose UP TO ONE from this group
- **Tier 2**: Choose your other 2 (or all 3 from Tier 2)

**Important**: Once you scan into your FIRST Lightning Lane at the park, **TIERS NO LONGER APPLY!** You can then book ANY ride.

**Loophole**: You can even MODIFY prebooked Tier 2 rides to Tier 1 rides after scanning into your first LL!

## Prebooking Strategy

### Booking Windows
- **On-site resort guests**: 7 days in advance
- **Off-site guests**: 3 days in advance
- **Window opens**: 7:00 AM ET sharp
- **Can book**: Entire length of stay (up to 14 days)

### Prebooking Order of Priority
Book parks in this order (highest demand first):
1. **Hollywood Studios** - Slinky Dog Dash sells out fastest!
2. **Magic Kingdom** - Tiana's, Peter Pan book fast
3. **EPCOT** - Remy's goes quickly
4. **Animal Kingdom** - ALWAYS BOOK LAST (lowest demand)

### Prebooking Best Practices

1. **Be ready at 7:00 AM ET sharp** - Especially for Slinky Dog Dash
2. **Book quickly first, modify later** - Lightning Lanes stay in cart for 5 minutes
3. **Book early return times** - You want to start scanning and churning through LLs early!
4. **Don't hoard late times** - If all your LLs are afternoon, you can't book more until then

**Ideal Prebooking Example (Magic Kingdom)**:
- Winnie the Pooh: 9:00-10:00am
- Haunted Mansion: 9:45-10:45am
- Tiana's Bayou Adventure: 10:45-11:45am

### What to Prebook by Park

**Magic Kingdom**: Tiana's (Tier 1) + Winnie the Pooh + Haunted Mansion or Pirates (Tier 2)

**EPCOT**: Remy's or Test Track (Tier 1) + Soarin' + Spaceship Earth (Tier 2)

**Hollywood Studios**: Slinky Dog Dash (Tier 1) + Toy Story Mania + Tower of Terror (Tier 2)

**Animal Kingdom**: Kilimanjaro Safaris + Expedition Everest + Na'vi River Journey

## The Refresh Hack (CRITICAL STRATEGY!)

The Refresh Hack is the **#1 strategy** for Lightning Lanes but is often overlooked!

### What It Does
- Find better/earlier time slots
- Snag "sold out" attractions
- Modify current selections to closer times

### How to Refresh

**Method 1: Drag Down**
- From the Wait Times screen, drag down to refresh
- Watch for times to change
- Click IMMEDIATELY when you see a good time

**Method 2: Click Through (More Reliable)**
- Click the time slot showing
- See what actual time appears on next screen
- That time is LOCKED IN once you get to confirmation screen
- If not what you want, go back and try again

### Critical Rules for Refreshing

1. **Times change in MILLISECONDS** - If you see a good time, click IMMEDIATELY
2. **Target ONE attraction** - Set it as your only "Top Pick" so it's at the top
3. **Lock in first, modify second** - Grab any time for a ride, THEN refresh to improve it
4. **Easier in morning** - Best refresh success before 10am
5. **Drops happen at specific times**:
   - Weather changes (rain!)
   - Parade/fireworks times
   - Meal times
   - When attractions have been going down often

### When Times Pop Up More Frequently
- Morning (before 10am) - BEST time to refresh
- During/after rain
- Around parade and fireworks
- During meal times
- Afternoons and evenings for cancellations

**Warning**: Refreshing can be addicting! Set a time limit (few minutes) and don't let it consume your vacation.

## Day-Of Strategy

### After Scanning Into First Lightning Lane

Once you scan into your FIRST LL:
1. **Tiers no longer apply!** Book ANY ride you want
2. **Can modify prebooked Tier 2s to Tier 1s** (possible loophole - may change)
3. **Can book for ANY park** (if park hopping)
4. **Immediately book your next LL** - Don't wait!

### The "Rolling 3" System
- You can hold up to 3 Lightning Lanes at a time
- Each time you USE one, book another immediately
- Goal: Churn through LLs throughout the day

### Book Early LLs to Scan Early
**Strategy**: Book at least one Tier 2 LL for early morning (even if not your top priority) specifically to SCAN IN and unlock:
- Ability to book ANY ride (no tiers)
- Ability to modify other LLs to Tier 1s
- Ability to book for other parks

## Grace Periods & Loopholes

### Lightning Lane Grace Period
- **Scan in up to 5 minutes EARLY**
- **Scan in up to 2 HOURS LATE**
- Example: 1:30-2:30pm window = can actually scan 1:25pm-4:30pm

### Advanced Stacking Loophole
Once a Lightning Lane hits the end of its window, it "expires" and you become eligible to book another - **even if you haven't used it yet!**

**This means you can hold MORE than 3 Lightning Lanes!**

Example: You have Mad Tea Party (expired) + Haunted Mansion (expired) + Tiana's + Space Mountain = 4 LLs! And both expired ones still work with the 2-hour grace period.

## Lightning Lane Single Pass Strategy

### Options for LLSP Attractions

1. **Buy the Single Pass** - Most convenient, just pay and skip
2. **Early Entry** - If you have the benefit, use it! (arrive early)
3. **End-of-Night Strategy** - Queue right before park close

### End-of-Night Strategy (HIGHLY RECOMMENDED)

You can queue for attractions **until the minute the park closes** - and actual wait will be **30-50% of posted wait!**

**Example**: Hollywood Studios closes at 9pm. Rise of the Resistance shows 45 min wait. Got in line at 8:52pm. **Actual wait: 8 minutes!**

**Risk**: If ride goes down, you're out of luck. But this is rare (maybe 3 times out of hundreds).

### Single Pass Booking Strategy

- **Prebook if you know you want it** - More availability
- **Return times after 4-5pm recommended** - Use mornings for standby/Multi-Pass
- **Check throughout day** - Times pop up from cancellations
- **Morning of (7am-9am) is easier** - More availability released

### Sell-Out Order (Hardest to Easiest)
1. Seven Dwarfs Mine Train (Magic Kingdom) - Hardest
2. Rise of the Resistance (Hollywood Studios)
3. TRON (Magic Kingdom)
4. Guardians of the Galaxy (EPCOT)
5. Flight of Passage (Animal Kingdom) - Easiest

## Park Hopping with Lightning Lanes

### How It Works
1. **Prebook LLs at your FIRST park** (up to 3)
2. **Scan into ONE LL at first park**
3. **Immediately book LLs for ANY park** - Tiers don't apply!
4. **Can modify remaining prebooked LLs to other parks**

### Pro Strategy
- Start at Animal Kingdom in morning with LLMP for Safari, Everest, Na'vi River Journey
- Scan into first LL
- Start booking LLs at Hollywood Studios or Magic Kingdom for afternoon/evening
- Makes for a GREAT day!

## Troubleshooting & Tips

### Dining Reservation Conflicts
During prebooking, Disney won't offer times that overlap with reservations. Solution:
1. Finish prebooking with whatever times work
2. Go back and MODIFY Lightning Lane times after
3. Day-of modifications are usually easier

### Where to Find Lightning Lanes in App
- Home screen → "Purchase" under Lightning Lane
- Three-bar menu → "Lightning Lane Passes"
- "My Plans" tab
- "Wait Times & Showtimes" (formerly Tip Board)

### Edit "Top Picks"
- Keep 1-3 selections in Top Picks throughout the day
- Puts your target attractions at top of screen
- Essential for refresh strategy!

### If Something Goes Wrong
Head to a **Blue Umbrella** (Guest Service booth) in the parks. Cast Members are excellent at resolving Lightning Lane issues quickly!

### Third-Party Tools
**Thrill-Data's Wait Magic** - Service that can find, book, and modify Lightning Lanes for you. Highly recommended if you don't want to constantly refresh!

Disney releases batches of Lightning Lane times throughout the day (not guest cancellations). These are usually available to use within the next hour!

**Note**: Times are speculative and not guaranteed. If ride is down or capacity is low, drops may not happen. Keep refreshing!

## FAQ

**Q: Can I buy Multi-Pass and Single Pass together?**
A: Yes! They're separate products. You can have both, just one, or neither.

**Q: Do I need Multi-Pass to buy Single Pass?**
A: No! They're independent. You can buy just a Single Pass for Flight of Passage without buying Multi-Pass.

**Q: Can I get LLs for only some people in my party?**
A: Yes! It's all a la carte. Pick days, parks, and people.

**Q: What about DAS (Disability Access Service)?**
A: DAS uses Lightning Lane queue but is its own service. You usually don't need LLMP if using DAS (except maybe at Magic Kingdom during very busy times).

**Q: What about Rider Swap?**
A: Works at Lightning Lane. Tell Cast Member at queue entrance you're doing Rider Swap.

**Q: Can I change my Multi-Pass to a different park?**
A: Yes! You can modify and pay the difference. (No refund if "downgrading" to cheaper park.)

**Q: Do I get unlimited rides with Multi-Pass?**
A: No - one Lightning Lane per ride, per day. For re-rides, use standby.

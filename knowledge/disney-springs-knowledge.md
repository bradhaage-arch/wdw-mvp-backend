## OVERVIEW

### What is Disney Springs?
Disney Springs is Walt Disney World's massive outdoor shopping, dining, and entertainment complex. It's FREE to enter (no park ticket required) and open to everyone - resort guests and the general public alike.

### Quick Facts
- **Location:** Eastern edge of WDW property
- **Size:** 120+ establishments across 4 neighborhoods
- **Hours:** Typically 10am-11pm (restaurants may vary)
- **Parking:** FREE in multiple garages
- **Cost to Enter:** FREE (you pay for what you buy/eat)

### The 4 Neighborhoods
1. **Marketplace** - Family-friendly, World of Disney store, classic Disney
2. **The Landing** - Waterfront dining, upscale restaurants
3. **Town Center** - Shopping focused, modern retail
4. **West Side** - Entertainment (AMC, Bowling, Cirque), House of Blues

## GETTING TO DISNEY SPRINGS

### From Disney Resorts
- **Bus:** Complimentary buses from all Disney resorts
- **Boat:** From Saratoga Springs, Old Key West, Port Orleans (French Quarter & Riverside)
- **Walking:** Saratoga Springs has a walking bridge

### From Off-Property
- **Car:** Free parking in Orange, Lime, and Grapefruit garages
- **Rideshare:** Dedicated Uber/Lyft drop-off zones

### Parking Tips
- **Orange Garage:** Closest to Town Center and The Landing
- **Lime Garage:** Closest to Marketplace and World of Disney
- **Grapefruit Garage:** Closest to West Side
- **Arrive before 5pm** on weekends to avoid parking headaches
- **Remember your level and section!** Garages are huge

## BEST RESTAURANTS

### Hard-to-Get Reservations (Book Early!)

**The BOATHOUSE**
- Cuisine: Seafood, steaks, American
- Vibe: Upscale waterfront, boat collection
- Must-Try: Filet, lobster tail, onion rings
- Pro Tip: Walk-up at the bar for same menu, no reservation

**Morimoto Asia**
- Cuisine: Pan-Asian (Japanese, Chinese, Korean)
- Vibe: Modern, dramatic space
- Must-Try: Peking duck, ribs, sushi
- Pro Tip: Morimoto Asia Street Food next door for quick bites

**Wine Bar George**
- Cuisine: Small plates, wine-focused
- Vibe: Upscale casual, Master Sommelier owned
- Must-Try: Crispy mac & cheese bites, cheese board, wine flights
- Pro Tip: Great for adults, one of the best wine selections in Orlando

**Frontera Cocina**
- Cuisine: Mexican (Rick Bayless)
- Vibe: Upscale Mexican, bright and modern
- Must-Try: Guacamole, tacos, margaritas

**Jaleo**
- Cuisine: Spanish tapas (José Andrés)
- Vibe: Lively, shareable plates
- Must-Try: Paella, jamón, gin & tonics

### Easier to Get (Still Great!)

**Raglan Road**
- Cuisine: Irish pub fare
- Vibe: Energetic, live music and dancers
- Must-Try: Fish & chips, bread pudding, Guinness
- Pro Tip: Live entertainment is included - very fun atmosphere!

**The Polite Pig**
- Cuisine: BBQ, Southern
- Vibe: Casual counter service with cocktails

**Homecomin'**
- Cuisine: Southern comfort (Chef Art Smith)
- Vibe: Cozy, farm-to-table
- Must-Try: Fried chicken, Church Lady deviled eggs, hummingbird cake

**Chef Art Smith's Homecomin' - Shine**
- Quick service window attached to Homecomin'
- Great for fried chicken sandwich on the go

**Paddlefish**
- Cuisine: Seafood
- Vibe: Boat-shaped restaurant, rooftop bar
- Must-Try: Lobster guacamole, crab cakes

### Quick Service Gems

- French fries with global toppings

## MUST-GET TREATS & SNACKS

### Gideon's Bakehouse 🍪
**THE hottest spot at Disney Springs**
- Famous for: Half-pound cookies, cake slices
- Flavors: Original chocolate chip, peanut butter crunch, seasonal specials
- **THE CATCH:** Virtual queue only, often full by morning

**Gideon's Strategy:**
1. Check the app RIGHT when Disney Springs opens (10am)
2. Join the virtual queue immediately
3. Your return window could be hours later
4. Return during your window, wait in a shorter line
5. Worth it? Most say YES - these cookies are unreal

### Amorette's Patisserie
- Location: The Landing
- Famous for: Petit cakes shaped like Disney characters
- Must-Try: Character dome cakes, macarons
- Great for: Instagram-worthy treats, special occasions

### Sprinkles

- Famous for: Cupcakes, Cupcake ATM (24/7!)
- Must-Try: Red velvet, seasonal flavors

### Salt & Straw

### Vivoli il Gelato

### Wetzel's Pretzels

## SHOPPING HIGHLIGHTS

### World of Disney
- **Location:** Marketplace
- **What it is:** Largest Disney store in the world
- **Why visit:** Overwhelming selection of Disney merchandise
- **Pro Tip:** Less crowded in the morning, absolute zoo in evenings

### The LEGO Store
- **Location:** Marketplace
- **Why visit:** Massive LEGO displays, pick-a-brick wall
- **Pro Tip:** Free monthly mini-builds (check schedule)

### Marketplace Co-Op
- **Location:** Marketplace
- **What it is:** Trendy Disney merchandise boutiques under one roof
- **Why visit:** More unique/adult Disney items than World of Disney
- **Shops inside:** Cherry Tree Lane, Twenty Eight & Main, Disney Centerpiece, etc.

### Star Wars Galactic Outpost
- **Location:** West Side
- **Why visit:** Star Wars merch outside of Galaxy's Edge

### Disney's Days of Christmas
- **Location:** Marketplace
- **Why visit:** Year-round Christmas ornaments and décor
- **Pro Tip:** Personalization available on many ornaments

### Basin
- **Location:** Marketplace
- **Why visit:** Bath bombs, soaps, lotions - Mickey-shaped bath bombs!

### Sugarboo & Co.
- **Location:** The Landing
- **Why visit:** Vintage-inspired gifts, signs, home décor
- **Great for:** Unique souvenirs beyond typical Disney

### Chapel Hats
- **Location:** Town Center

### Uniqlo
- **Location:** Town Center

### shopDisney Outlet (Character Warehouse)

## ENTERTAINMENT

### Cirque du Soleil: Drawn to Life
- **Location:** West Side
- **What:** Disney-themed Cirque du Soleil show
- **Duration:** ~90 minutes
- **Cost:** $$$$ (separate ticket required)
- **Worth it?:** If you love Cirque, absolutely. Stunning visuals.

### AMC Disney Springs 24
- **Location:** West Side
- **What:** Dine-in movie theater with 24 screens
- **Features:** MacGuffins bar, Fork & Screen dine-in, Dolby Cinema
- **Pro Tip:** Great rainy day activity!

### Splitsville Luxury Lanes
- **Location:** West Side
- **What:** Upscale bowling alley with restaurant/bar

- **Location:** West Side

- **Raglan Road:** Live Irish music and dancers inside (free with dining)

## DISNEY SPRINGS STRATEGIES

### Best Time to Visit
- **Weekday mornings:** Lightest crowds
- **Weekend evenings:** Most crowded, parking difficult
- **During park hours:** Less crowded than after parks close
- **Rainy days:** Expect crowds as park-goers redirect here

### When to Use Disney Springs
1. **Arrival Day:** Dinner here when you land
2. **Rest Day:** Shop, eat, and relax with no park stress
3. **Last Day:** Before heading to airport (stores open at 10am)
4. **Rainy Day:** Covered areas, movie theater, indoor activities
5. **Dining Reservations:** When parks are sold out of ADRs

### Dining Reservation Hack: OpenTable
Many Disney Springs restaurants are on **OpenTable** with BETTER availability than My Disney Experience:
- The BOATHOUSE
- Morimoto Asia
- Paddlefish
- STK Orlando
- Wine Bar George
- And more!

**Always check OpenTable** if MDE shows no availability.

### Budget Tips
- **Eat here vs. parks:** Often better value, bigger portions
- **Lunch vs. dinner:** Some restaurants have cheaper lunch menus
- **Happy hours:** Several restaurants offer them
- **Share desserts:** Portions are typically huge
- **Skip the chains:** You can eat at Rainforest Cafe anywhere

### With Kids
- **LEGO Store:** Let them play with the displays
- **Marketplace Carousel:** Classic carousel ride
- **Splash pads:** Small water play areas for cooling off
- **Disney's Days of Christmas:** Magical year-round
- **T-REX:** Dinosaur-themed restaurant (like Rainforest Cafe)

## SAMPLE DISNEY SPRINGS ITINERARIES

### Afternoon/Evening Visit (4-5 hours)
1. Arrive ~4pm, park in Orange Garage
2. Walk through Town Center, browse shops
3. Early dinner reservation at The Landing (~5:30pm)
4. Post-dinner stroll to Marketplace
5. World of Disney shopping
6. Gideon's cookies (if you got a virtual queue)
7. Walk the waterfront back to garage

### Full Day Rest Day
1. Sleep in! Arrive ~11am
2. Browse Town Center shops
3. Lunch at Polite Pig or D-Luxe Burger
4. Catch a movie at AMC (afternoon showtime)
5. Coffee break at Joffrey's
6. More shopping at Marketplace
7. Early dinner at Raglan Road (enjoy live music!)
8. Dessert from Amorette's
9. Evening waterfront stroll

### Quick Dinner Stop (2 hours)
1. Have backup reservation at Disney Springs if park dining falls through
2. Arrive, park close to restaurant location
3. Enjoy dinner
4. Quick walk through nearest shops
5. Grab dessert to go
6. Back to resort for early night

## PRO TIPS

1. **Mobile Order works** at some quick-service locations
2. **Stroller rentals** available if you need one
3. **Package pickup** - Some purchases can be sent to your resort (Disney resort guests)
4. **Don't try to do it all** - It's massive, pick your priorities
5. **Comfortable shoes** - You'll walk a lot on hard surfaces
6. **Bring a jacket at night** - Waterfront gets breezy
7. **Take the boat if you can** - More relaxing than bus, scenic
8. **Check for construction** - Disney Springs is always evolving
9. **Gift cards** - Disney gift cards work here, can buy discounted from Target/Sam's Club
10. **PhotoPass photographers** sometimes station here for free Magic Shots

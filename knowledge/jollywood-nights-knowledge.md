## EVENT OVERVIEW

### What is Jollywood Nights?
Jollywood Nights is a separately-ticketed, after-hours holiday event at Disney's Hollywood Studios. It's Disney's answer to bringing a special holiday party experience to Hollywood Studios, similar to Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party at Magic Kingdom.

### Event Basics
- **Location:** Disney's Hollywood Studios
- **When:** Select nights in November and December
- **Hours:** Typically 8:30pm - 12:30am (4 hours)
- **Entry Time:** Wristband distribution and entry begins at 7:00pm
- **Capacity:** Limited tickets sold per night

### Ticket Information
- Separately ticketed event (not included with regular park admission)
- Prices vary by date (weekend/closer to Christmas = more expensive)
- Tickets often sell out for popular dates
- Can enter Hollywood Studios as early as 7:00pm with event ticket (no day ticket required)

## EXCLUSIVE ENTERTAINMENT

### Jollywood Nights Projections & Fireworks
The park features special holiday projection shows and pyrotechnics throughout the evening, including projections on the Chinese Theatre and Tower of Terror.

### Holiday Character Cavalcades
Special character cavalcades travel down Hollywood Boulevard throughout the night featuring beloved Disney characters in holiday attire.

### Stage Shows & Performances
- Live entertainment stages throughout the park
- Holiday-themed musical performances
- DJ dance parties

### Snowfall on Hollywood Boulevard
Magical "snow" falls on Hollywood Boulevard throughout the evening, creating a winter wonderland atmosphere in Florida.

## ATTRACTIONS

### Attraction Availability
Most Hollywood Studios attractions operate during Jollywood Nights with typically shorter wait times than regular park hours.

**Usually Available:**
- Tower of Terror
- Rock 'n' Roller Coaster
- Slinky Dog Dash
- Toy Story Mania
- Alien Swirling Saucers
- Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway
- Star Tours
- Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run
- Rise of the Resistance (when operational)

**Check Day-Of:**
- Some attractions may have modified hours or closures
- Rise of the Resistance availability can vary

### Attraction Strategy
- **First Hour:** Lines are often longest 8:30-9:30pm as everyone rushes in
- **Mid-Event:** 10:00-11:00pm often has reasonable waits
- **Last Hour:** 11:30pm-12:30am typically has shortest waits
- **Use this time for headliners** you couldn't get during the day

## SAMPLE JOLLYWOOD NIGHTS ITINERARY

### Before the Event
- **Afternoon:** Consider entering Hollywood Studios with a regular day ticket to enjoy the park
- **6:45pm:** Head toward the front of the park
- **7:00pm:** Pick up your wristband at distribution points
- **7:00-8:30pm:** You can stay in the park with your event wristband (day guests will be cleared out)

### Event Night Strategy

#### 8:30pm - 9:30pm (First Hour)
- Enjoy the opening moments on Hollywood Boulevard
- Watch the first snowfall
- Grab a holiday drink/snack
- Get in line for your #1 priority character (if characters are important to you)
- OR ride a headliner attraction right at 8:30pm while others watch entertainment

#### 9:30pm - 10:30pm (Mid-Event)
- Hit attractions while moderate crowds
- Catch character cavalcades on Hollywood Boulevard
- Enjoy stage entertainment
- Take photos with holiday decorations and projections

#### 10:30pm - 11:30pm (Sweet Spot)
- Character lines start to decrease
- Catch any characters you missed
- Ride more attractions (lines getting shorter)
- Enjoy another round of snowfall

#### 11:30pm - 12:30am (Final Hour)
- **Shortest attraction waits** of the night
- Hit any remaining must-do rides
- Soak in the atmosphere
- Last chance for photos and treats
- Stay until the very end for the lightest crowds

## PRO TIPS

### Ticket Strategy
1. **Book early** - Popular dates sell out
2. **Weeknights are cheaper** and less crowded than weekends
3. **Early December** dates tend to be less crowded than mid-December
4. **Avoid the last event date** before Christmas - typically most crowded

### What to Wear
- **Layers!** Florida evenings can get chilly in December (50s-60s°F)
- **Comfortable shoes** - You'll be walking for 4+ hours
- **Holiday attire welcome** - Many guests wear festive clothing
- **Glow accessories** - Fun for the nighttime atmosphere

### What to Bring
- **Portable phone charger** - You'll be using the app and taking lots of photos
- **Light jacket or sweater**
- **Autograph book** if meeting characters
- **Wallet/MagicBand** for food and merchandise purchases

### Photography Tips
- **Hollywood Boulevard** looks stunning with projections and snow
- **Tower of Terror** with holiday projections = great photo op
- **Chinese Theatre** projections are worth capturing
- **Sunset Boulevard** decorations photograph well
- **Best photos:** 9:00-10:00pm before it gets too crowded, or after 11:30pm

### Money-Saving Tips
- **Eat dinner before the event** - Food is not included like MVMCP
- **Skip the day ticket** - Enter at 7pm with just your event ticket
- **Choose a weeknight** for lower ticket prices
- **Set a treat budget** - Easy to overspend on specialty items

## IS JOLLYWOOD NIGHTS WORTH IT?

### Best For:
- Hollywood Studios fans who want a holiday experience
- Those who've done MVMCP and want something different
- Guests who want shorter attraction wait times
- Adults who enjoy holiday cocktails and atmosphere
- Star Wars fans wanting Galaxy's Edge at night during the holidays

### May Not Be Worth It For:
- Families expecting free treats like MVMCP
- Those primarily wanting a parade experience
- Guests on a tight budget (food costs add up)
- Anyone who absolutely needs the "classic" Disney Christmas experience (MVMCP is more traditional)

### The Verdict
Jollywood Nights is a **solid holiday event** but different from MVMCP. It's less about parades and free cookies, more about atmosphere, attractions, and holiday vibes at Hollywood Studios. If you love HS and want to experience it in a holiday setting with lower crowds, it's worth considering. If you can only do ONE holiday event, most guests prefer MVMCP for the more complete Christmas experience.

## PARK HOPPING WITH LIGHTNING LANE

### How It Works
After scanning into just ONE attraction at your first park, you can then book any attraction (no tiers apply) for the next park, at any time. You can even change any prebooked Tier 2s to LLs for the next park after scanning into only one!

### Example Strategy
Starting at Hollywood Studios but hopping to EPCOT that evening:
1. Scan into first LL at HS
2. Immediately book Frozen Ever After at EPCOT (before it sells out - it's a hard one to get!)
3. Use remaining 2 rolling Lightning Lanes at Hollywood Studios until you don't need them anymore
4. Then book more at EPCOT

### App Tips for Park Hopping
- You need to tell the app you're park hopping to see "Top Picks" for your second park
- Things can be glitchier when using the app to park hop, but it's doable
- Don't worry about booking a LL early morning for an evening time slot at another park
- You can still manage "churning" attractions throughout the day without all 3 slots

### Multi Experience Pass (When Rides Break Down)
When a ride you have a LL for breaks down, you receive a Multi Experience Pass:
- **Where to find it:** Usually only shows up in "My Plans" (not always obvious after initial notification)
- **Always good for:** The initial ride you had booked
- **Can also be redeemed at:** Other attractions (sometimes a huge win if you booked an easy ride!)
- **Park hopping:** Can usually redeem at certain attractions at any other park (may need to talk to Guest Services at a blue umbrella)
- **Important:** Once issued, it no longer counts as one of your three Lightning Lanes - you're eligible to book another immediately!

## SAMPLE ITINERARY #1: FAMILIES & EARLY BIRDS

**Best For:** Parents with Toddlers, Families, Early Birds
**Ticket Type:** One park per day with a planned break day

---

### Day 1: Magic Kingdom

#### MORNING
- Purchase the Lightning Lane Multi Pass for the day (always worth it at Magic Kingdom)
- Get to the park for Early Entry/Rope Drop
- Hit as many Fantasyland attractions as you can that are < 20-minute waits
- Hit Adventureland/Frontierland/Liberty Square right after

**Best Morning Treats:**
- Waffles from Sleepy Hollow
- Cheshire Cat Tail from Cheshire Cafe

#### MID-DAY
- Break back to the hotel for a swim or nap
- If staying in the park: do a sit-down lunch reservation, shows, parade, and character greetings to conserve energy

**Parade Viewing Tips:**
- Front of the park near the Flagpole = great and easy spot
- Alternative: Watch near the Hall of Presidents in Liberty Square

#### EVENING
- Head back to the park ~4pm
- Hit Tomorrowland and any other Fantasyland attractions missed earlier with standby or LLs

**Dinner Options:**
- Cosmic Ray's with younger kids (sit in main room with Sonny, the singing animatronic)
- Pecos Bill with older kids
- Mobile Order recommended (check times about an hour before you want to eat!)

**Evening Fireworks:**
- Grab treats at Main Street Confectionery or Plaza Ice Cream
- Watch from mid-back of Main Street for fewer crowds AND to see the projections

**After Fireworks:**
- Hit more attractions (low waits and easy LL refreshing!)
- OR get family photos on Main Street
- DON'T leave right after fireworks - avoid the initial exit mob

---

### Day 2: Animal Kingdom

#### MORNING
**Two Strategy Options:**
1. Hit attractions early morning → skip LLMP → take mid-day break
2. Get LLMP → sleep in → stay until evening to experience Pandora after dark!

#### MID-DAY
- Break at pool OR stay in park
- Character brunch or lunch at Tusker House
- **Brunch Hack:** Book one of the latest breakfast slots to get BOTH breakfast and lunch spreads!
- Indoor (AC!) shows: Finding Nemo or Lion King
- Take the train to Rafiki's Planet Watch if you have toddlers
- Cocktail/tea/coffee break at Nomad Lounge for the parents (reader favorite!)

#### EVENING
**Quick-Service Dinner:**
- Satuli Canteen
- Flame Tree Barbecue (try the Baked Mac & Cheese!)

**Experience Pandora After Dark:**
- Hit Flight of Passage or Navi River Journey at end of night for much shorter wait than posted

**Alternative:** Animal Kingdom day makes for a good resort evening - head back early for dinner and swimming or nighttime activities at the hotel

---

### Day 3: Break Day - Take It Easy

**Top Options:**
1. Water Park or pool day and activities at the resort
2. **Resort-Hopping Day:** Head to the Boardwalk and ride surrey bikes, have breakfast or dinner at Trattoria al Forno, grab margaritas, shop & enjoy the live entertainment
3. Character breakfast at Topolino's at Riviera Resort (pair with option #2!)
4. Lunch at Beaches & Cream (hit Boardwalk after if desired)

**Dinner at Disney Springs:**
- Boathouse or Raglan Road

**Dessert at Disney Springs:**
- Cookies from Gideon's
- Cupcakes from Sprinkles
- Visit Goofy's Candy Co.
- Bougie desserts from Amorette's Patisserie

---

### Day 4: Hollywood Studios

#### MORNING
- Highly recommend using the Multi Pass here
- Consider a mid-day break in or out of the park if you're going to try to make it to Fantasmic! in the evening
- Hit Toy Story Land or Galaxy's Edge in the morning
- Consider late character breakfast at Hollywood & Vine if the kids are Disney Jr. lovers
- Make sure to grab those Lightning Lanes for the afternoon and evening throughout the day

#### MID-DAY
**Sit-Down Lunch:**
- Sci-Fi Dine-In (favorite)
- 50's Prime Time Cafe (favorite)

**Quick-Service Alternatives:**
- Docking Bay 7 in Galaxy's Edge
- ABC Commissary
- PizzeRizzo

**Afternoon Activities:**
- Hit afternoon shows (Indiana Jones and Frozen Sing-Along are favorites!)
- Attempt to modify + refresh for better Lightning Lane times during downtime
- Explore Galaxy's Edge: shops, build a Droid or Lightsaber (get a reservation!), try Blue & Green Milks

#### EVENING
- Hit attractions with Lightning Lanes or shorter waits
- Enjoy dinner at one of the lunch spots listed above

**Watch Fantasmic!**
- If there are two seatings, the latter will be less crowded
- You shouldn't need to get there too far in advance (if at all) to get a seat

**Star Wars fans:** Enjoy Galaxy's Edge after dark if possible!

---

### Day 5: EPCOT

#### MORNING
- If you can get the group up early to hit some headliners, you can likely skip the Lightning Lane Multi Pass
- Otherwise, sleep in and buy it!

**Early Entry/Rope Drop Strategy:**
- Hit at least one of these: Remy's Ratatouille Adventure or Frozen Ever After
- Hit any other attractions in the front of the park while waits are short
- Save any longer ones for the evening

**Then:**
- Head to the World Showcase for lunch and to tour around the world
- Hit Kidcot stops or do the current Festival Scavenger Hunt for little ones (super fun)

#### MID-DAY
**Best Food Picks:**
- La Cantina in Mexico
- Fish & Chips from the UK
- Regal Eagle Barbecue from America

- Break back to the hotel for a swim or nap if desired
- Make sure to hold those evening Lightning Lanes if using the pass!

#### EVENING
- Start back in the front of the park
- Hit any attractions you may have missed in the morning (waits will be shorter due to most people being in the World Showcase)

**Sit-Down Dinner in World Showcase (Top Picks for Families):**
- Via Napoli
- San Angel Inn
- Biergarten

**Dessert:**
- Grab dessert from France (Les Halles pastry case or crepes!)

**Evening Finale:**
- Watch the nighttime show
- OR hit Frozen or Remy's end of night if missed this morning

## SAMPLE ITINERARY #2: ADULTS & PARK HOPPERS

**Best For:** Adults, Wine/Diners, Girls Trips, Solo Trips
**Ticket Type:** Park Hopper

---

### Day 1: Magic Kingdom + EPCOT

#### MORNING
- Purchase the Lightning Lane Multi Pass (always worth it at Magic Kingdom)
- Get to the park for Early Entry/Rope Drop
- Hit as many Fantasyland attractions as you can that are < 20-minute waits
- Hit Adventureland/Frontierland/Liberty Square after

**Early Lunch at Skipper Canteen** (reservation recommended, but sometimes they take walk-ups)
- Love: the noodles, fish, "secret" cheese bread appetizer, and all of the cocktails!

#### MID-DAY
- Hit rides & shows until ~2/3pm

**Planning for EPCOT Hop:**
- Make sure to grab a Lightning Lane for EPCOT once finished with LLs at Magic Kingdom

**Optional Monorail Hop:**
- Go for cocktails at Enchanted Rose (Grand Floridian) or Tiki Terrace (Polynesian) before heading to EPCOT
- Plan for this to take ~1.5 hours total

**Take Monorail to EPCOT** (listen for the switch at TTC station)

#### EVENING
- Hit the World Showcase
- Use Lightning Lanes to try and get Frozen or Remy's (use LL Masterclass hacks!)

**Snack and Drink Around the World:**
- Fish & Chips and pub blends in the UK
- Margaritas and nachos in Mexico
- Grand Marnier slushies & crepes in France

**Evening Finale:**
- Watch the nighttime show
- OR get in the Frozen Ever After or Remy's queue before park close (actual wait should be only about 30% of posted!)

---

### Day 2: EPCOT + Hollywood Studios

#### MORNING
- Get the Lightning Lane Multi Pass (means you can sleep in and not rush)
- Book early LL return times for EPCOT
- Once you scan into the first one or two, grab a good evening Lightning Lane for Hollywood Studios (like Slinky Dog Dash or Rock 'n' Roller Coaster)
- Hit Future World rides using both standby + Lightning Lanes

#### MID-DAY
**Lunch in World Showcase** (whatever was missed from last night!)

**Sit-Down Lunch Recommendations:**
- San Angel Inn (top pick - one of the coolest restaurants at WDW)
- Chefs de France

- Make sure to start booking Lightning Lanes for Hollywood Studios!
- Plan to hop to Hollywood Studios around 4:30/5pm - take the ferryboat or Skyliner

**Optional:** Take the Skyliner to Riviera for coffee break + pastry at Le Petit Cafe (on the way to Hollywood Studios)

#### EVENING
- Use your Lightning Lanes to hit attractions

**See Fantasmic!** (the early showing if there are two)

**Hit Rise of Resistance immediately after Fantasmic!** for a short wait (again, it will be much shorter than posted!)

**Head to Oga's Cantina after park close** (get a reservation or try walk-up!)
- Enjoy having an almost empty park to yourself when you leave!

---

### Day 3: Animal Kingdom + Dinner at Animal Kingdom Lodge

**Either:** Hit attractions early morning → skip LLMP → take mid-day break
**OR:** Get LLMP → sleep in → stay until evening for Pandora at night!

#### MORNING
Hit attractions on the right side of the park that may have lower standby waits:
- Dinosaur
- Expedition Everest
- Kali River Rapids

#### MID-DAY
- Break at pool OR stay at park
- Cocktail/tea/coffee break at Nomad Lounge (join the walk-up list in the app about ~60 minutes before)
- Sit-down shows: Festival of the Lion King, Finding Nemo, or the bird show in Asia

**Best Snacks:**
- Buffalo Chicken Chips at Trilo-Bites
- Seasoned Fries at Mr. Kamal's
- Curry Corn on the Cob in Africa

- Grab snacks and hit the animal trails in Asia and Africa

#### EVENING
**Pro tip:** Take the bridge from Africa to Pandora at dusk if possible - serious tranquility!

- Experience Pandora after it gets dark
- Hit Flight of Passage or Navi River Journey at end of night for a short wait

**Take a bus to Animal Kingdom Lodge to dine at Jiko or Sanaa** to finish off your evening. Totally worth the trip!

---

### Day 4: Hollywood Studios + EPCOT

#### MORNING
- You'll want the Lightning Lane Pass again (unless you're cool with just doing shows and standby)
- Make sure to prebook a LL for Slinky Dog Dash
- Use rope drop/Early Entry to hit Tower of Terror and Rock 'n' Roller Coaster (if lines are manageable!)
- Get morning coffee from the Joffrey's at the exit of Tower of Terror
- Start refreshing/booking more Lightning Lanes
- Hit Star Tours, then see Indiana Jones or Frozen Sing-Along (yes, even adults - it's dry and hilarious)

**Early Lunch at ABC Commissary** - get that Buffalo Chicken Grilled Cheese!

#### MID-DAY
- Explore the shops and stalls of Galaxy's Edge
- Do Single Rider line for Smugglers Run if desired

**Break for drinks and pretzels (with beer cheese...)** at Baseline Tap House

#### EVENING
- Hop to EPCOT around 5pm - take the ferryboat or Skyliner

**Sit-Down Dinner in World Showcase (Top Dinner Picks):**
- Biergarten
- San Angel Inn
- Rose & Crown

**Evening Finale:**
- See the nighttime show
- OR go enjoy a ride on Soarin' or Test Track at the end of the night!

---

### Day 5: Magic Kingdom

#### MORNING
- LLMP again unless you're good with some lines or more casual attractions in the middle of the day
- Rope drop Magic Kingdom or sleep in

**Optional:** Brunch at Grand Floridian Cafe
- Top picks: Chicken and Waffles or Lobster Thermidor Burger!

#### MID-DAY
- Hit shows and attractions not done on the first day

**Easy and Chill Adult Attractions:**
- PeopleMover
- Carousel of Progress
- Hall of Presidents
- Just hanging on Main Street USA (make sure to see the Dapper Dans!)

- Get Dole Whips if you haven't yet (favorite: Pineapple-Raspberry Swirl Float)
- Consider monorail hopping if you haven't done it yet (if you only make one stop, highly recommend the Polynesian)

#### EVENING
**Hop on the monorail for dinner at California Grill with the fireworks**
- Make sure to time your reservation right so you're there during the show!

**Alternative if you don't have a reservation:**
- Hit the Steakhouse 71 Lounge area
- Get the Stack Burger
- (This option is a lot less of a dent on your wallet!)

## KEY ITINERARY INSIGHTS

### When to Buy Lightning Lane Multi Pass

| Park | Buy LLMP? | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Magic Kingdom | YES - Always worth it | Highest value |
| Hollywood Studios | YES - Highly recommend | Especially for Slinky Dog Dash |
| EPCOT | Depends | Can skip if you rope drop headliners |
| Animal Kingdom | Optional | Can skip if you hit attractions early morning |

### End-of-Night Strategy (Both Itineraries)
- Actual wait times are typically only **30-50% of posted wait** near park close
- Best rides to hit end-of-night:
  - Frozen Ever After (EPCOT)
  - Remy's Ratatouille Adventure (EPCOT)
  - Rise of the Resistance (after Fantasmic!)
  - Flight of Passage (Animal Kingdom)
  - Navi River Journey (Animal Kingdom)

### Best Times to Park Hop
- Leave first park around 2-3pm for mid-day hop
- Leave first park around 4:30-5pm for evening hop
- Skyliner and ferryboat are great options between EPCOT and Hollywood Studios

### Must-Do Restaurant Experiences

| Restaurant | Type | Why It's Great |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Skipper Canteen | Lunch | Noodles, fish, "secret" cheese bread, cocktails |
| San Angel Inn | Lunch/Dinner | One of the coolest restaurants at WDW |
| Nomad Lounge | Break | Perfect cocktail/tea/coffee break |
| California Grill | Dinner | Fireworks from private observation deck |
| Jiko/Sanaa | Dinner | Worth the trip to Animal Kingdom Lodge |
| Topolino's | Breakfast/Dinner | One of the best meals on property |
| Oga's Cantina | After Park Close | Empty park when you leave! |

### Break Day Ideas (Both Itineraries)
1. Water Park or pool day
2. Resort-Hopping Day (Boardwalk, Riviera, monorail resorts)
3. Character breakfast at Topolino's
4. Disney Springs dinner and dessert (Gideon's cookies!)
5. Beaches & Cream lunch

### Pro Tips Across Both Itineraries
- Mobile Order food about an hour before you want to eat
- Join walk-up lists ~60 minutes before at lounges like Nomad Lounge
- After fireworks is great time for Main Street photos
- Galaxy's Edge after dark is special (especially for Star Wars fans)
- The later Fantasmic! showing is less crowded
- Build a Droid or Lightsaber requires a reservation

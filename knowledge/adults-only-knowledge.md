## WHY DISNEY WITHOUT KIDS?

### It's Not Just for Families
Disney World is increasingly popular with adults traveling without children. Here's why:

- **World-class dining** - Seriously good restaurants
- **Incredible bars** - Trader Sam's, Oga's Cantina, wine bars
- **Thrill rides** - No height requirements limiting you
- **Leisurely pace** - Sleep in, stay out late, no meltdowns
- **Festivals** - EPCOT festivals are designed for adults
- **Theming & details** - Appreciate the artistry without chasing kids
- **Nostalgia** - Relive childhood memories your way

### Best Times for Adult Trips
- **EPCOT Festivals** - Food & Wine (fall), Flower & Garden (spring)
- **Weekdays** - Fewer families with school-age kids
- **Marathon Weekend (January)** - Active adult crowd
- **Swan & Dolphin Food & Wine Classic** - Separate ticketed event

## THE MONORAIL BAR CRAWL

### The Classic Adult Disney Experience
Ride the monorail loop, stopping at each resort's best bar. This is a WDW rite of passage for adults.

### The Route
The monorail connects: **Magic Kingdom → Contemporary → Ticket & Transportation Center → Polynesian → Grand Floridian → back to Magic Kingdom**

### The Bars (In Suggested Order)

**Stop 1: The Polynesian**

**Trader Sam's Grog Grotto** ⭐ MUST DO
- **Vibe:** Immersive tiki bar with "show" when you order certain drinks
- **Why it's special:** Drinks trigger special effects - the room comes alive!
- **Must-order:** Uh-Oa!, Krakatoa Punch, Nautilus
- **Pro tip:** Opens at 3pm, line forms early. Or try walk-up later (gets easier after 8pm)
- **Tip:** If Grog Grotto is full, Trader Sam's Tiki Terrace outside has same drinks, no wait

**Tambu Lounge**
- **Vibe:** Relaxed, lobby-adjacent bar
- **Why it's special:** Same appetizer menu as 'Ohana without the reservation!
- **Must-order:** Lapu Lapu, 'Ohana Pot Stickers, Pulled Pork Sliders
- **Pro tip:** Great backup if Trader Sam's line is too long

---

**Stop 2: Grand Floridian**

**Enchanted Rose** ⭐ MUST DO
- **Vibe:** Elegant Beauty and the Beast themed lounge
- **Why it's special:** Upscale cocktails, gorgeous theming, live piano some evenings
- **Must-order:** The Grey Stuff (dessert drink!), classic cocktails, cheese board
- **Pro tip:** Walk-ups usually available, especially on weekdays

**Citricos Lounge**
- **Vibe:** Wine bar meets Mediterranean elegance
- **Why it's special:** Quieter alternative, excellent wine selection
- **Good for:** A more sophisticated, less themed experience

---

**Stop 3: Contemporary**

**Steakhouse 71 Lounge** ⭐
- **Vibe:** Mid-century modern, full food menu available
- **Why it's special:** Great cocktails AND you can eat a full meal without a reservation
- **Must-order:** Stack Burger, Whiskey Sour, Bacon-Cheese Dip
- **Pro tip:** You can watch Magic Kingdom fireworks from the patio!

**Outer Rim**
- **Vibe:** Casual lobby lounge
- **Why it's special:** Views of the monorail passing through the building
- **Good for:** Quick drink, people watching

**California Grill Lounge** (if you can get up there)
- **Vibe:** Fine dining lounge, top floor
- **Why it's special:** Best fireworks views on property
- **Pro tip:** Need a reservation OR sometimes they allow walk-ups at the lounge. Ask!

---

### Monorail Crawl Tips
1. **Start at Polynesian around 4pm** - Beat Trader Sam's rush
2. **Eat dinner at one of the lounges** - Steakhouse 71 Lounge is perfect
3. **Pace yourself** - These drinks are STRONG
4. **Plan 3-4 hours** for the full crawl
5. **End at Contemporary** for Magic Kingdom fireworks views
6. **Take monorail back to TTC** and Uber home if needed

## THE SKYLINER BAR CRAWL

### An Alternative to the Monorail Crawl
The Skyliner connects several resorts near EPCOT and Hollywood Studios.

### The Route
**EPCOT → BoardWalk/Yacht & Beach Club (walk) → Riviera → Caribbean Beach → Hollywood Studios**

### The Bars

**EPCOT Starting Point**
- Grab a drink in World Showcase before heading out
- **La Cava del Tequila (Mexico)** - Best margaritas on property
- **UK Beer Cart** - Pub drinks
- **France** - Wine, champagne

**BoardWalk Area** (walk from EPCOT International Gateway)

**AbracadaBar** ⭐
- **Vibe:** Speakeasy, magic themed
- **Why it's special:** Hidden cocktail lounge, prohibition era magic theme
- **Must-order:** The Conjurita, Coney Island Cure

**Belle Vue Lounge**
- **Vibe:** Old-time radio era, cozy
- **Why it's special:** Quiet, overlooked, vintage cocktails
- **Good for:** Chill drink with less crowds

**BoardWalk Joe's Marvelous Margaritas**
- Outdoor walk-up window
- Great for: Quick margarita to walk around with

---

**Riviera Resort** (Skyliner from EPCOT or BoardWalk station)

**Bar Riva** ⭐
- **Vibe:** Poolside European cafe
- **Why it's special:** Mediterranean cocktails, great food, vacation vibes
- **Must-order:** Aperol Spritz, riviera cocktails

**Topolino's Terrace Lounge**
- **Vibe:** Rooftop, upscale
- **Why it's special:** Best views, often has walk-up bar seating
- **Must-order:** Wine, Italian cocktails
- **Pro tip:** Restaurant is hard to book but bar takes walk-ups!

**Le Petit Cafe**
- **Vibe:** Coffee bar with pastries and drinks
- **Why it's special:** Boozy coffees, French pastries
- **Good for:** Afternoon pick-me-up

---

**Caribbean Beach**

**Banana Cabana** ⭐
- **Vibe:** Tropical pool bar
- **Why it's special:** Caribbean vibes, frozen drinks, laid-back atmosphere
- **Must-order:** Pirate's Paradise, Caribbean Rum Punch

---

### Skyliner Crawl Tips
1. **Start at EPCOT World Showcase** for drinks + dinner
2. **Walk to BoardWalk** from EPCOT back entrance
3. **Take Skyliner to Riviera** - Beautiful at sunset
4. **End at Caribbean Beach** or head to Hollywood Studios
5. **Skyliner stops running** around 10:30pm - plan accordingly

## LOUNGE DINING HACKS

### Skip the Hard-to-Get Reservation, Eat at the Lounge

Many restaurants have lounges that serve the same (or similar) food with no reservation needed!

| Restaurant | Lounge | What You Get |
|------------|--------|--------------|
| **'Ohana** | Tambu Lounge | Pot stickers, wings, bread pudding - same menu! |
| **Topolino's Terrace** | Topolino's Lounge | Apps and drinks, no character breakfast |
| **California Grill** | California Grill Lounge | Full menu at bar (if you can get up there) |
| **Steakhouse 71** | Steakhouse 71 Lounge | Full menu including famous Stack Burger |
| **Narcoossee's** | Lounge seating | Same menu, fireworks views |
| **Toledo** | Lounge area | Spanish tapas without reservation |
| **Jiko** | Jiko Lounge | African-inspired appetizers |
| **Sanaa** | Sanaa Lounge | Bread service and small plates! |

### Pro Tips for Lounge Dining
1. **Arrive right when lounge opens** for best seat selection
2. **Ask if full menu is available** - Some lounges offer limited menus
3. **Expect to wait** if it's a popular spot - but no reservation needed
4. **Tip well** - Bartenders remember good tippers
5. **Don't hover** during busy times - finish and give up your seat

## DRINKING AROUND THE WORLD (EPCOT)

### The Classic Adult EPCOT Experience
Visit all 11 World Showcase countries, have a drink in each.

### The Countries & What to Drink

| Country | Signature Drink | Pro Tip |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| **Mexico** | Margarita (La Cava del Tequila) | Skip the outdoor stand, go INSIDE to La Cava for the best |
| **Norway** | Viking Coffee, Aquavit | Kringla Bakeri has boozy coffee drinks |
| **China** | Plum Wine, Tsing Tao | Joy of Tea kiosk |
| **Germany** | Beer flight, Schöfferhofer | Beer garden atmosphere |
| **Italy** | Wine, Limoncello | Via Napoli bar or wine walks |
| **America** | Craft beer, Bourbon | Regal Eagle or Fife & Drum |
| **Japan** | Sake, Suntory Whiskey | Kabuki Cafe slushies are amazing |
| **Morocco** | Moroccan Mint Tea (boozy) | Spice Road Table |
| **France** | Grand Marnier Slush, Wine | Les Vins de France, L'Artisan des Glaces (boozy ice cream!) |
| **UK** | Pub ales, Cider | Rose & Crown Pub |
| **Canada** | Moosehead, Ice Wine | Le Cellier Lounge |

### Strategies

**Full 11-Country Crawl:**
- Start at Mexico (left side from entrance)
- Pace yourself - this is 11 drinks!
- Share drinks with your group
- Eat food along the way (IMPORTANT)
- Takes 4-6 hours comfortably

**Mini Crawl (5-6 Countries):**
- Pick your favorites
- Better pacing
- More time to enjoy each stop

**Best of the Best (If Short on Time):**
1. La Cava del Tequila (Mexico) - Best margaritas
2. Germany Beer Garden - Best atmosphere
3. Japan - Best sake and slushies
4. UK - Best pub experience
5. France - Best wine/slushies

### Pro Tips
1. **Eat before you start** - Or eat along the way
2. **Stay hydrated** - Florida heat + alcohol = trouble
3. **Don't rush** - Enjoy the theming
4. **Do it during a Festival** - Even more food/drink options!
5. **Get the Festival passport** if available - Track your progress

## BEST ADULT DINING EXPERIENCES

### Splurge-Worthy Fine Dining

**Victoria & Albert's** (Grand Floridian)
- Disney's ONLY AAA Five Diamond restaurant
- 10+ course tasting menu
- Jackets required for gentlemen
- $$$$$$ - Worth it for special occasions

**California Grill** (Contemporary)
- Top of the Contemporary
- Fireworks views
- Modern American, excellent sushi
- Request fireworks seating

**Takumi-Tei** (EPCOT Japan)
- Upscale Japanese
- Wagyu beef, kaiseki options
- Beautiful atmosphere

**Topolino's Terrace** (Riviera)
- Rooftop Italian
- Handmade pasta, Riviera views
- Brunch has characters (avoid for adult experience)

**Morimoto Asia** (Disney Springs)
- Iron Chef Masaharu Morimoto
- Peking duck, sushi, pan-Asian

### Romantic Dinner Options
- **Narcoossee's** - Fireworks views, waterfront
- **Yachtsman Steakhouse** - Classic steakhouse, intimate
- **Citricos** - Mediterranean, elegant
- **Tutto Gusto** - Intimate Italian wine cellar (walk-ups only!)
- **Le Cellier** - Canadian steakhouse, cozy
- **San Angel Inn** - Mexico pyramid, unique atmosphere

### Fun/Unique Experiences
- **Space 220** - "Dining in space" above EPCOT
- **Oga's Cantina** - Star Wars bar (reservations!)
- **Sci-Fi Dine-In** - Eat in "cars" watching old movies
- **50's Prime Time Cafe** - "Mom" scolds you if you don't eat your veggies
- **Beaches & Cream** - Kitchen sink sundae challenge

## THRILL RIDES WITHOUT KIDS

### The Best Part of Adult Trips
You can hit ALL the big rides without height restrictions or scared kids!

### Tier 1 - Don't Miss
- **Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind** (EPCOT) - Reverse launch coaster
- **Tron Lightcycle Run** (MK) - Fastest Disney coaster
- **Rise of the Resistance** (HS) - Best immersive experience ever
- **Flight of Passage** (AK) - Truly incredible, fly on a banshee
- **Expedition Everest** (AK) - Great coaster, backwards section

### Tier 2 - Excellent
- **Tower of Terror** (HS) - Classic drop ride, terrifying theming
- **Rock 'n' Roller Coaster** (HS) - Launch coaster, Aerosmith soundtrack
- **Slinky Dog Dash** (HS) - Surprisingly fun for adults
- **Test Track** (EPCOT) - Design your car, feel the speed
- **Space Mountain** (MK) - Classic, in the dark

### Single Rider Lines
Skip the wait without Lightning Lane:
- **Test Track** - Significant time savings
- **Rock 'n' Roller Coaster** - When available
- **Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run** - Very effective

## ADULT-FOCUSED RESORTS

### Best Resorts for Adults Without Kids

**The Vibe You Want:**

**Polynesian Village**
- Best bar on property (Trader Sam's)
- Beautiful, romantic grounds
- Magic Kingdom monorail access
- Fireworks views from beach

**BoardWalk Inn**
- Walk to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios
- Nightlife on the BoardWalk itself
- AbracadaBar, Belle Vue Lounge on-site
- Classic Atlantic City theming

**Grand Floridian**
- Most elegant resort
- Enchanted Rose bar
- Spa, fine dining
- Quiet sophistication

**Riviera Resort**
- European elegance
- Topolino's Terrace
- Beautiful rooftop bar
- Skyliner access

**Animal Kingdom Lodge**
- Incredible theming
- Jiko, Sanaa, Boma restaurants
- Animals on the savanna (wine + giraffes!)
- Away from the chaos

### Resorts to Avoid for Adult Trips
- **Art of Animation** - Family suites, very kid-focused theming
- **All-Star Resorts** - Budget, very family/kid-oriented
- **Pop Century** - Nostalgic but crowded with kids

## SPAS & RELAXATION

### Resort Spas

**Senses Spa at Grand Floridian**
- Full-service spa
- Couples treatments available
- Pools, saunas, relaxation areas

**Senses Spa at Saratoga Springs**
- Similar offerings to Grand Floridian
- Usually easier to book

**Mandara Spa at Dolphin**
- Balinese-inspired
- Great for couples

### Pool Days
- Adult-only pools don't exist at Disney, BUT...
- **Quiet pools** at deluxe resorts are much less chaotic
- **Weekday mornings** = fewer kids at any pool
- **Riviera** pool area tends to be more adult-friendly atmosphere

## ADULT TRIP SAMPLE ITINERARY (5 Days)

### Day 1: Arrival + Disney Springs
- Arrive, check into resort
- Explore resort bars/lounges
- Dinner at Disney Springs (Morimoto Asia or The BOATHOUSE)
- Drinks at Wine Bar George

### Day 2: EPCOT + Drinking Around the World
- Late morning start (sleep in!)
- Guardians of the Galaxy (Lightning Lane Single Pass)
- Start World Showcase at 11am opening
- Drink around the world (5-6 countries)
- Dinner reservation at Takumi-Tei or Space 220
- Nighttime show

### Day 3: Monorail Bar Crawl + Magic Kingdom Evening
- Morning at resort pool
- Monorail Bar Crawl starting at 4pm (Trader Sam's → Enchanted Rose → Steakhouse 71)
- Hop to Magic Kingdom for evening
- Ride Tron, Space Mountain, Big Thunder
- Fireworks

### Day 4: Hollywood Studios + Boardwalk
- Rope drop Rise of the Resistance
- Tower of Terror, Rock 'n' Roller Coaster, Slinky
- Lunch at Docking Bay 7 + Oga's Cantina reservation
- Walk to BoardWalk
- AbracadaBar cocktails
- Dinner at Flying Fish or Trattoria al Forno

### Day 5: Animal Kingdom + Resort Evening
- Morning Safari (animals are most active)
- Flight of Passage
- Nomad Lounge for drinks and apps (EXCELLENT)
- Return to resort
- Dinner at resort or nearby resort restaurant
- Fireworks from resort (if MK view available)

## PRO TIPS FOR ADULT TRIPS

1. **Sleep in sometimes** - You're adults, you don't need rope drop every day
2. **Make lounge reservations** - Oga's Cantina, Tutto Gusto, etc.
3. **Uber/Lyft is your friend** - Don't rely only on Disney transportation after drinking
4. **Dress up** - Some restaurants have dress codes, and it's fun
5. **Extended Evening Hours** - Stay at a Deluxe resort for extra park time with fewer kids
6. **Eat at the bar** - Often better service and no reservation needed
7. **Try EVERYTHING** - No picky kids limiting restaurant choices
8. **Pace your drinking** - It's easy to overdo it in the Florida heat
9. **Take photos** - You're making memories too, not just for Instagram
10. **Embrace the magic** - Being an adult at Disney is its own special experience

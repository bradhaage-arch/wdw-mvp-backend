# EPCOT Knowledge Base

## Park Overview

EPCOT is the only Disney World park with **two entrances**:
1. **Front Entrance** - Main entrance near Spaceship Earth. Buses, monorail, and parking access here.
2. **International Gateway (World Showcase Entrance)** - Back entrance in World Showcase. Access from Skyliner, EPCOT-area resorts (BoardWalk, Yacht/Beach Club, Swan/Dolphin), and Hollywood Studios park hoppers.

**Key insight**: Where you're staying/entering from dictates your rope drop strategy. This is critical for Early Entry planning.

EPCOT is a **BIG park** - the World Showcase alone is 1.2 miles around. Limited shade, can get very hot. Plan for significant walking.

### Park Layout
- **World Celebration** - Spaceship Earth, CommuniCore Hall (character meets)
- **World Discovery** - Guardians of the Galaxy, Test Track, Mission Space
- **World Nature** - Soarin', Living with the Land, The Seas, Journey of Water, Journey Into Imagination
- **World Showcase** - 11 country pavilions circling the lagoon (Mexico → Canada clockwise)

## Rope Drop & Arrival Strategy

### By Entrance Location

**From Front Entrance (buses, monorail, driving):**
- Best for: Guardians of the Galaxy, Test Track
- If Guardians is priority: Be first in line, ride at rope drop
- If Test Track is priority: Head there for Early Entry

**From International Gateway (Skyliner, EPCOT resorts, HS hoppers):**
- Best for: Remy's Ratatouille Adventure, Frozen Ever After
- Strategy: Remy's first, then Frozen (often can do both during EE without long waits)

### Arrival Time Recommendations

| Priority | Arrival Before EE |
|----------|-------------------|
| Test Track, Guardians, or Remy's | 30 minutes before Early Entry |
| Frozen Ever After | 15 minutes before Early Entry |
| General touring (slower time of year) | Can arrive later |
| Busy time of year | Double the above times |

### Transport Timing
- **Skyliner**: Starts ~1 hour before Early Entry (sometimes 45 min)
  - From Pop/Art of Animation: 5-min ride + transfer at Caribbean Beach Hub (12 min from there)
- **Buses**: Start ~1 hour before Early Entry
- **EPCOT resorts walking**: 5-8 minutes from Yacht/Beach/BoardWalk. Swan/Dolphin is longer (consider ferry)
- **Driving**: Parking opens 1 hour before Early Entry. Allow 20 minutes from parking gate to inside park

### Getting Through Security
- International Gateway: Very quick, usually just a few minutes
- Front entrance before 11am: Allow ~15 minutes for walk + security

## Touring Strategies

### Strategy 1: Front Half First (Recommended for Ride-Focused)
1. Morning: Hit rides in World Celebration/Discovery/Nature
2. Afternoon break (optional)
3. Late afternoon/evening: World Showcase touring, dinner, fireworks
4. End of night: Any missed headliners (waits drop significantly)

### Strategy 2: Two Laps (For Explorers)
1. Morning: Half of front-of-park rides
2. Midday: World Showcase tour
3. Evening: Return to front for remaining rides
4. Night: Back to World Showcase for dinner/fireworks
- **Note**: More walking, but more relaxed pace

### Strategy 3: World Showcase First (Late Arrivals)
1. Start in World Showcase (most opens at 11am)
2. Lunch, explore pavilions when crowds are lighter
3. After 4pm: Head to front of park (wait times drop significantly)
4. Evening: Rides have shorter waits

### Key Timing Insight
**The front of the park REALLY slows down after 4-5pm.** If a standby line is too long during the day, come back in the evening.

## Lightning Lane Strategy

### With Lightning Lane Multi Pass

**Tier 1 Options** (pick ONE to pre-book):
- Frozen Ever After
- Remy's Ratatouille Adventure  
- Test Track

**Tier 2 Options**:
- Soarin'
- Spaceship Earth
- Mission Space
- Journey Into Imagination
- Living with the Land
- The Seas with Nemo & Friends
- Turtle Talk with Crush

**Pro Strategy**:
1. Pre-book whichever Tier 1 is most important
2. Book ANY Tier 2 for first thing in the morning (even if no wait)
3. Scan into that first LL as early as possible
4. Immediately modify your other Tier 2 bookings → Tier 1 (Frozen, Remy's, Test Track) before they sell out
5. Check at 1:47pm for afternoon availability that opens up

### Without Lightning Lane Multi Pass
- **Must use Early Entry** to avoid long waits
- **Plan for one long wait** during the day
- **End-of-night strategy**: Get in line last 10-20 minutes - actual wait is often 50% or less than posted
- **Single Rider** (Test Track, Remy's): Age 7+, party splits up, but dramatically shorter waits

### Lightning Lane Single Pass
- **Guardians of the Galaxy**: Highly recommend purchasing if budget allows
  - Best return time: ~10:30-11:30am
  - The ride is arguably the best at Disney World
  - Makes your day much easier

2. Meet Anna & Elsa (Norway) - get in line when it opens

4. Front of park: Journey Into Imagination, Living with the Land, Seas with Nemo, SeaBase
5. Turtle Talk with Crush
6. **Break time**: Club Cool (try sodas), playground outside Club Cool, Joffrey's giant donuts
7. Meet Mickey & Friends
8. Journey of Water
9. World Showcase afternoon: KidCot stops, characters, festival scavenger hunt
10. Dinner: Via Napoli, Teppan Edo, San Angel Inn (kid-friendly), or Garden Grill/Akershus (character dining)
11. Fireworks OR skip fireworks and ride Frozen/Remy's during show

## Pro Tips

### General Tips
1. **Two entrances** - Your strategy changes based on where you're entering from
2. **Lightning Lane Multi Pass** makes life easier but you CAN do EPCOT without it if you use Early Entry and strategic timing
3. **Front of park clears out after 4-5pm** - Evening is great for rides
4. **Single Rider lines** (Test Track, Remy's) - Age 7+, dramatically reduces waits
5. **Soarin' waits are hyperinflated** - Posted 40 min often = actual 20 min
6. **World Showcase opens at 11am** (most of it) - Don't rush there at rope drop

### For Young Kids
- Festival scavenger hunts + KidCot = sanity savers (~$12 investment worth it)
- Playground outside Club Cool is great for a break
- Best bathrooms: Norway
- ImageWorks play area after Figment is a nice break
- SeaBase aquarium can kill time while kids explore

### Money Savers
- Skip Guardians LLSP if budget is tight - rope drop from front or end-of-night standby works
- Don't need LL Multi Pass if willing to use Early Entry and wait for 1-2 rides
- Festival food can replace a sit-down meal (graze around the world)

### Hidden Gems
- **Club Cool** - Free international Coca-Cola samples
- **SeaBase** - Underrated aquarium, great for a break
- **Japan pavilion** - Matsuriza drummers, Mitsukoshi store is incredible
- **Morocco pavilion** - Beautiful to explore, often overlooked
- **Canada Far & Wide** - Circle-Vision film, rarely crowded
- **Living with the Land** - Relaxing, air-conditioned, interesting

## Sample Itineraries

### Full Day with Lightning Lane (Adults/Older Kids)

1. **Pre-trip**: Purchase Guardians LLSP (~10:30-11:30am), pre-book Tier 1 LL
2. **Early Entry** (Front): Test Track or Guardians | (Int'l Gateway): Remy's → Frozen
3. Scan into first LL immediately, then modify Tier 2 → Tier 1 for Frozen/Remy's/Test Track
4. If didn't pre-book Soarin' or Mission Space, ride standby (short morning waits)
5. Continue booking/scanning LLs: Journey Into Imagination, Soarin', Living with the Land, Seas with Nemo, Spaceship Earth, Mission Space
6. Journey of Water (15-20 min)
7. SeaBase exploration
8. Guardians (ideally before/around lunch, or come back evening)
9. Club Cool
10. Mickey & Friends meet (optional)
11. **World Showcase afternoon**: Explore, snack, check entertainment times in MDE app
12. Favorites: Mexico pyramid + La Cava margarita + Gran Fiesta Tour, China shop, Karamell-Küche (Germany), America Gardens Theater, Japan drummers & exhibits, Morocco, France (everything), Rose & Crown (UK), Canada Far & Wide

4. Test Track Single Rider (age 7+, will split party)

7. Living with the Land
8. Seas with Nemo + SeaBase
9. Journey of Water

14. Return to front of park ~4:30pm - Guardians if haven't done, festival booths, missed rides

18. **World Showcase evening**: Remy's standby often dips ~6/6:15pm dinner rush
19. **Nighttime show** OR last 10 min of park - get in line for headliner (actual wait = 50% or less of posted!)

2. **Early Entry** (Int'l Gateway): Remy's → Frozen | (Front): Frozen → Soarin'

## Key Reminders

- **EPCOT is big** - Minimize backtracking, plan your route
- **Two entrances** - Strategy depends on where you enter
- **Tier 1 LL trick** - Scan into ANY LL early, then modify Tier 2 → Tier 1
- **Single Rider** - Test Track & Remy's, age 7+, huge time saver
- **After 4-5pm** - Front of park clears out dramatically
- **End of night** - Posted waits are inflated, actual often 50% less
- **1:47pm** - Check for LL availability that opens up
- **World Showcase opens 11am** - Don't rope drop to the back unless doing Frozen/Remy's
- **Festivals** - Scavenger hunts are worth it for families with kids

## RESORT RANKINGS OVERVIEW

Rankings based on three criteria (not factoring budget/value):
- Location/Transportation (out of 10)
- Amenities/Food (out of 10)  
- Theming/Rooms (out of 10)

### TOP TIER RESORTS (23-25 points)

#### 1. Polynesian Village Resort - 25 points
- Location/Transportation: 7/10
- Amenities/Food: 9/10
- Theming/Rooms: 9/10

**Pros:** Gorgeous and immersive theming, great food, proximity to Magic Kingdom, best bar on property (Trader Sam's)

**Cons:** Grounds are confusing, further away from parks that aren't Magic Kingdom

---

#### 2. BoardWalk Inn & Villas - 24 points
- Location/Transportation: 8/10
- Amenities/Food: 7/10
- Theming/Rooms: 9/10

**Pros:** Immersive theming, more amenities than any other resort, walking distance to two parks and close bus ride to other two

**Cons:** Quick/easy food choices can be difficult to find

---

#### 3. Yacht Club / Beach Club - 24 points
- Location/Transportation: 9/10
- Amenities/Food: 7/10
- Theming/Rooms: 8/10

**Pros:** Classy and nostalgic theming, best pool on property (Stormalong Bay), walking distance to two parks and close bus ride to other two

**Cons:** Quick/easy food choices can be difficult to find

---

#### 4. Animal Kingdom Lodge - 23 points
- Location/Transportation: 5/10
- Amenities/Food: 8/10
- Theming/Rooms: 10/10

**Pros:** Best theming of all resorts, some of the best sit-down restaurants on property, animals!

**Cons:** Buses only to the parks and further away from some of them, food options in the Kidani building super limited

---

#### 5. Riviera Resort - 23 points
- Location/Transportation: 8/10
- Amenities/Food: 8/10
- Theming/Rooms: 7/10

**Pros:** Clean, modern grounds, great pools and outdoor recreation options, easy Skyliner access to two parks, delicious food options

**Cons:** Theming is a little lacking

---

### MID TIER RESORTS (20-22 points)

#### Coronado Springs - 22 points
- Location/Transportation: 6/10
- Amenities/Food: 8/10
- Theming/Rooms: 8/10

**Pros:** Amazing restaurant options and beautiful grounds, central location

**Cons:** Resort is spread out, buses only with four bus stops, quick/easy food options can feel limited

---

#### Swan/Dolphin - 22 points
- Location/Transportation: 8/10
- Amenities/Food: 7/10
- Theming/Rooms: 7/10

**Pros:** Close to two parks, amazing pools, lots of food options, rooms quieter than a lot of hotels

**Cons:** No Disney theming, some bus options can be slow/MK bus drops off at TTC

---

#### Grand Floridian - 21 points
- Location/Transportation: 7/10
- Amenities/Food: 6/10
- Theming/Rooms: 8/10

**Pros:** Gorgeous and elegant theming, diverse food options, good pools

**Cons:** Sometimes doesn't feel like much is going on here, quick/easy food options can get limited, further away from parks that aren't Magic Kingdom

---

#### Contemporary Resort - 21 points
- Location/Transportation: 7/10
- Amenities/Food: 8/10
- Theming/Rooms: 6/10

**Pros:** Next to Magic Kingdom, great dining options, nice rooms

**Cons:** Building is old and there's no real theme, further away from parks that aren't Magic Kingdom

---

#### Pop Century - 21 points
- Location/Transportation: 8/10
- Amenities/Food: 6/10
- Theming/Rooms: 7/10

**Pros:** Skyliner access to two parks, buses to others come frequently, fun theming

**Cons:** Limited food options, bus lines can be long in the morning, kids might not find theming exciting

---

#### Wilderness Lodge - 21 points
- Location/Transportation: 6/10
- Amenities/Food: 7/10
- Theming/Rooms: 8/10

**Pros:** Immersive and unique theming, fun amenities, great pool bar/grill (Geyser Point)

**Cons:** Transportation options limited to buses or boat (that can often take too long/get too crowded)

---

#### Art of Animation - 20 points
- Location/Transportation: 8/10
- Amenities/Food: 6/10
- Theming/Rooms: 6/10

**Pros:** Themed/bigger rooms & suites, Skyliner access, better food court than other Value resorts, one of the best pools for toddlers

**Cons:** Theming can be cheesy and the grounds are a bit sterile

---

### LOWER TIER RESORTS (17-19 points)

#### Port Orleans French Quarter - 19 points
**Pros:** Fun theming and nice grounds, beignets!, fun pool and waterslide
**Cons:** Limited food options, buses only and further away from the parks, resort feels outdated

#### Old Key West - 19 points
**Pros:** Relaxing "Old Florida" theming, lots of recreational activities, beautiful grounds
**Cons:** Grounds are spread out, buses only with five stops and further from the parks, limited food options

#### Saratoga Springs - 18 points
**Pros:** Theming is minimal but upscale, beautiful grounds, fun main pool and recreational offerings
**Cons:** Grounds are spread out, buses only with five stops and further from the parks, limited food options

#### Port Orleans Riverside - 18 points
**Pros:** Pretty grounds, fun amenities, clean/newer rooms
**Cons:** Limited food options, buses only with four stops and further away from the parks, theming isn't that exciting

#### Caribbean Beach - 18 points
**Pros:** Skyliner access, some parts of the grounds are pretty (the lake), some buildings are walking distance to Riviera giving more food options, amazing main pool & pool bar (Banana Cabana)
**Cons:** Grounds and some rooms feel quite outdated and rundown (motel vibes), bus system has EIGHT stops, limited food options

#### Fort Wilderness Resort - 18 points
**Pros:** Amazing immersive theming if it's what you're going for, lots more space, tons of random and unique recreational activities (could spend days here and not go to the parks)
**Cons:** Food options can feel limited, and to go to any of the parks will take more time than most other resorts

#### All Stars (Movies, Music, Sports) - 17 points
**Pros:** Some theming can be fun for kids, buses come frequently, amenities are fun
**Cons:** Some buildings are quite far from the main building and transportation, food options are limited

## ROOM REQUESTS - COMPLETE GUIDE

### Why Make Room Requests?
Most people traveling to Disney don't know this option exists. Even making a basic request can have your room ready early or spare you from being a far walk from the main area/transportation.

### Room Request Timeline
1. Figure out which request is best for you
2. Submit it **5-7 days before your trip**
3. Reinforce it at the resort if needed

### What's Realistic to Request
- **DON'T** request an "upgrade" or a type of room you didn't pay for
- If you paid for Standard Room, you probably won't get Preferred Room
- Unrealistic requests make you seem demanding and hurt your chances

### How to Submit Requests

**Option 1: Phone (Best but time-consuming)**
- Main number: (407) 939-4357
- Or call your specific resort directly

**Option 2: Chat Feature (Easiest)**
- Found on WDW website under Help & Rules > Contact Us
- Script: "Hi there! We have an upcoming stay at [RESORT NAME] and I'd love to submit a room request if that's possible."

**Option 3: In-Person at Front Desk**
- Must arrive early (before ~12pm)
- Best for reinforcing phone/chat requests

### Important Tips
- Submit online check-in BEFORE making room requests
- If arriving early and have your heart set on a specific request, skip Online Check-In and call/chat instead, then reinforce in person
- If using a Travel Agent, they must call in the request for you

---

### Generic Requests (Work at Any Resort)

1. **Top or higher floor and/or "outer perimeter" room** - Quieter, avoids noise from above. Warning: often means further walk.

2. **Ground floor** - Good if you have lots of stuff to tote each day

3. **"As close to XYZ as possible"** - Transportation, pool, lobby, etc. Almost always accommodated!

---

### Resort-Specific Room Requests

#### ALL-STAR MOVIES
- **Preferred Room:** Request Toy Story, 101 Dalmatians, or Fantasia section
- **Standard Room:** Request Mighty Ducks section (near quiet pool) or "as close to transportation/food court as possible"

#### ALL-STAR MUSIC
- **Preferred Room:** Request Calypso building (Building 1)
- **Standard Room:** Request Broadway Building 8

#### ALL-STAR SPORTS
- **Preferred Room:** Request Building 1 or 6 (close to pool and food court/buses)
- **Standard Room:** Request Building 9 (along football field perimeter)

#### ANIMAL KINGDOM LODGE
- **HACK for Standard View:** Request room with "partial Savannah view" - these are technically Standard but have Savannah views!
- **Savannah View:** Add request for close to transportation or pool
- Can request close to lobby or pool to minimize walking

#### ART OF ANIMATION
- **Suites:** Request your preferred theme (Finding Nemo, Cars, Lion King)
- **Finding Nemo:** Request Building 3 for closest to main area
- **Cars:** Request Building 3 for closest to main area
- **Lion King:** Request Building 6 for closer to Skyliner
- **Little Mermaid (Standard):** Request Lake View in Building 7

#### BEACH CLUB
- **Water View:** Request pool view (best pool on property!) or Crescent Lake
- **Villas:** Request "as close to EPCOT as possible"
- **Standard:** No specific recommendations, use generic requests

#### BOARDWALK
- **Villas:** Request Boardwalk view or close to elevator (avoids being way in the back)
- **Water View:** Request Crescent Lake view (way better than pool), ask for room above restaurants if possible
- **Standard:** Request based on preference - close to EPCOT or close to pool/lobby

#### CARIBBEAN BEACH
- **Close to Riviera:** Request Buildings 53-56
- **Closest to Skyliner:** Buildings 41-42 (Jamaica) or 54-56 (Aruba)
- **Middle ground:** Buildings 45-46 (Jamaica) or 51-52 (Aruba)
- **Closest to buses/main area:** Barbados or Martinique section

#### CONTEMPORARY/BAY LAKE TOWER
- Request quieter room in Tower if noise is a concern
- Request close to monorail for convenience
- Request "partial water view" for possible free upgrade
- **Bay Lake Tower:** Request Fifth Floor close to walkway (connects to Contemporary main area)

#### CORONADO SPRINGS
- **Tower room:** Request "as high a floor as possible"
- **Standard Room:** Request Building 8C in Cabanas (close to main pool, Tower, and bus stops)
- **Avoid:** The Ranchos section (furthest from everything)

#### FORT WILDERNESS
- **Cabins:** Request section 2800 (closest to Outpost Depot)

#### GRAND FLORIDIAN
- Close to monorail/buses/main building: Request Sugar Loaf Key 6 or Big Pine Key 9
- Close to beach/play areas: Request Big Pine Key Building 9
- Close to boat to MK: Request Conch Key Building 7
- Close to main pool: Request Boca Chica Building 8 or Big Pine Key Building 9
- **Villas:** Request Big Pine Key Building 9 (more central)

#### OLD KEY WEST
- **IMPORTANT:** Request Buildings 23-27 specifically to be near first bus stop and walking distance to main area
- This gives you multiple bus stop options and avoids 10-15 minute bus loop

#### POLYNESIAN
- **Standard Room:** Request Aotearoa, Fiji, or Tuvalu
- **Near pools:** Request Samoa
- **Water/Theme Park View:** Request Tuvalu
- **Villas:** Request Tokelau (closest to main area)

#### POP CENTURY
- **Preferred Room:** Request Building 4 or 5 in '60s section (closest to Skyliner, pool, and main area)
- **Standard Room:** Request Building 6 in '70s section (close to Skyliner) or Building 9 in '80s section (closest to buses/main area)

#### PORT ORLEANS FRENCH QUARTER
- Near pool: Request Building 2 or 5
- Near food court/buses: Request Building 4
- More removed/quiet: Request Building 1 or 7

#### PORT ORLEANS RIVERSIDE
- **Preferred Room:** Request Building 14 or 15
- **Standard Room:** Request Building 38 (near 2nd bus stop, walking path to pools)
- **Mansion buildings:** Request Building 85

#### RIVIERA
- Small resort, use generic requests
- Request specific floor (high or low), view of fireworks, or close to pool/Skyliner/cafe

#### SARATOGA SPRINGS
- **Walkers going to Disney Springs:** Request Congress Park area
- **Near main amenities:** Request The Springs section
- **Pool lovers:** Request The Paddock area, specifically 5000s building

#### WILDERNESS LODGE
- Close to lobby/buses: Request that specifically
- Close to boats/Geyser Point: Request that (good for adults or families spending time at MK)
- **Copper Creek:** Same requests apply
- **Boulder Ridge:** Request close to lobby (some Villas are far)

#### YACHT CLUB
- Close to EPCOT: State that in request
- Close to Hollywood Studios: State that in request
- Close to ferry boat: State that in request
- **Lagoon/Pool View:** If early to bed, request water view room AWAY from main pool
- Request view "directly facing the Marina and Boardwalk" for best water views

## RESORT HOPPING GUIDE

### How to Resort Hop
Disney buses do NOT go from resort to resort - you must transfer at a park or drive/take rideshare.

**EXCEPTIONS that DO connect resorts:**
- The Monorail Loop
- The Skyliner Routes

### Transportation Maps

#### Monorail Loop
Magic Kingdom → Contemporary → Transportation and Ticket Center (TTC) → Polynesian → Grand Floridian → back to Magic Kingdom

#### Skyliner Routes
- **Hub:** Caribbean Beach (Transfer Station)
- **Routes from Caribbean Beach:**
  - To/from Pop Century & Art of Animation
  - To/from Riviera
  - To/from EPCOT International Gateway
  - To/from Hollywood Studios

---

### TOP 3 RECOMMENDED RESORT HOPS

#### #1: BoardWalk
Disney considers this resort alone a "destination" at Walt Disney World. Can keep you entertained for hours!

**Easiest to get to from:** EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, any Skyliner resort

**BoardWalk Checklist:**
- [ ] Rent a surrey bike and bike around Crescent Lake
- [ ] Treat yourself from the pastry case at The Boardwalk Deli
- [ ] Classic cocktail at Belle Vue Lounge
- [ ] Rock (or nap) on the rocking chairs overlooking the Grand Lawn
- [ ] Hit the arcade
- [ ] Get nachos and a margarita around Crescent Lake
- [ ] Dine at one of the restaurants - Trattoria al Forno is a favorite
- [ ] Pizza Window for dinner (great family meal, best view in Disney!)
- [ ] Let the kids run around the "Green" lawn

---

#### #2: The Monorail Hop
Prioritize visiting the Polynesian & Grand Floridian. Contemporary is nice, but last on the list if time-crunched.

**Easiest to get to from:** Magic Kingdom, any monorail resort

**Polynesian Checklist:**
- [ ] Grab tiki drinks at Trader Sam's and/or the Tambu Lounge (Trader Sam's is one of the most unique bar experiences of your life - kids allowed until 8pm!)
- [ ] Explore the grounds - most beautifully landscaped resort in the World
- [ ] Check out the waterfalls outside the lobby
- [ ] Get a view of the Castle behind the pool
- [ ] Walk out to the beach area
- [ ] Shop at BouTiki for unique Polynesian Disney items
- [ ] Nosh on Pulled Pork Nachos from Captain Cook's
- [ ] Grab Dole Whips at Pineapple Lanai (often has unique flavors!)
- [ ] Splurge on dinner at Ohana

**Grand Floridian Checklist:**
- [ ] Catch the Grand Floridian Pianist in the lobby
- [ ] Have tea at the Garden View Tea Room (if available)
- [ ] Enjoy the lake view and pristine grounds
- [ ] Have a spa day at the Grand Floridian Spa
- [ ] Try Grand Floridian Cafe (Lobster Eggs Benedict, Madagascar Vanilla Brioche French Toast)
- [ ] Bougie cocktails at The Enchanted Rose

**Contemporary Checklist:**
- [ ] Book Chef Mickey's for classic character breakfast
- [ ] Dine at California Grill (time it for MK fireworks from private observation deck!)
- [ ] Seasonal desserts from Contempo Cafe pastry case
- [ ] Cocktail and Stack Burger from Steakhouse 71 lounge

---

#### #3: Animal Kingdom Lodge
A bit more out of the way (bus or car only), but worth it!

**Easiest to get to from:** Animal Kingdom park

**Animal Kingdom Lodge Checklist:**
- [ ] Pick up famous Zebra Domes from The Mara
- [ ] Animal observation on the Savannah (best at evening!)
- [ ] Check out kids activities in lobby: cookie decorating, face painting, learning drums (open to public!)
- [ ] Dine at Sanaa, Boma, or Jiko (consistently highest-rated restaurants on property)
  - Boma for breakfast
  - Sanaa and Jiko for dinner

---

### OTHER FAVORITE RESORT HOPS

#### Riviera Resort
**Easiest to get to from:** EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, any Skyliner resort

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Check out the beautiful Mosaic Murals (walk from Skyliner station)
- [ ] Hit Le Petit Cafe for pastries (one of the best sweet cases on property)
- [ ] Eat and drink at Bar Riva and/or Primo Piatto (try the Lemon Mascarpone pancakes or Baked Brie!)
- [ ] Dinner or character breakfast at Topolino's (one of the best meals on property - dinner often takes walk-ups in bar/lounge)

---

#### Caribbean Beach
**Easiest to get to from:** EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, any Skyliner resort

*Note: Doesn't have much for non-guests and not as nice as other resorts, but on Skyliner so including favorites*

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Banana Cabana pool bar (best pool bar at Disney - top notch vibes, great food menu!)
- [ ] Caribbean Cay - giant play area on the "island" for young kids

---

#### Wilderness Lodge & Fort Wilderness
**Easiest to get to from:** Magic Kingdom

**Wilderness Lodge Checklist:**
- [ ] Search for Hidden Mickeys (get tip sheet from front desk)
- [ ] Hit Geyser Point (gorgeous outdoor waterfront bar/grill - try the bison burger with marionberry jam)
- [ ] Take in the lobby (worth 10-15 minute tour alone)
- [ ] Watch the Geyser erupt (every hour, on the hour behind the pool)

**Fort Wilderness Checklist:**
- [ ] Non-stop recreation activities: kayaks, bikes, canoes, wagon rides, pony rides, fishing, boat rentals

---

### BAR CRAWL IDEAS

#### Monorail Bar Crawl
1. **Polynesian - Trader Sam's:** Campy AF tiki bar
2. **Polynesian - Tambu Lounge:** Tiki drinks, old-school lobby bar vibes
3. **Grand Floridian - Enchanted Rose:** Elegant, upscale/classic cocktails
4. **Contemporary - Steakhouse 71 Lounge:** Have a meal with your drink, get out of Magic Kingdom

#### Skyliner Bar Crawl
1. **BoardWalk - Abracadabar:** Speakeasy, dark, cocktails
2. **Riviera - Bar Riva:** Poolside, vacation vibes
3. **Riviera - Topolino's:** Elegant, wine
4. **Caribbean Beach - Banana Cabana:** Caribbean, tropical, tiki drinks

#### Skyliner Snack Hop
1. **BoardWalk - Boardwalk Deli** (don't be fooled by 'deli' - it's a whole bakery!)
2. **Riviera - Le Petit Cafe**

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

### Best Resorts By Category
- **Best Theming:** Animal Kingdom Lodge (10/10)
- **Best Location:** Yacht Club/Beach Club (9/10) or BoardWalk (8/10)
- **Best Pool:** Beach Club (Stormalong Bay)
- **Best Bar:** Polynesian (Trader Sam's)
- **Best Food Options:** Polynesian, Animal Kingdom Lodge, Contemporary
- **Best for Skyliner Access:** Pop Century, Art of Animation, Riviera, Caribbean Beach
- **Best Budget Option with Good Transportation:** Pop Century (Skyliner access!)

### Room Request Success Tips
1. Submit 5-7 days before check-in
2. Be realistic - don't ask for upgrades you didn't pay for
3. Be specific with building numbers when possible
4. Reinforce in person if arriving early
5. Generic requests like "close to transportation" have high success rates

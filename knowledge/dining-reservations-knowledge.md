# Dining & Reservations Knowledge Base

## Reservation Strategy

### Booking Windows
- **On-site guests**: 60 days in advance + entire length of stay (up to 10 days)
- **Off-site guests**: 60 days in advance, one day at a time
- **Opens**: 6:00 AM ET (sometimes earlier - be ready at 5:30 AM to be safe)

### The Ultimate Dining Hack (24-Hour Method)

**This is the #1 strategy for getting hard-to-get reservations!**

Almost every restaurant at Disney sees multiple cancellations starting the day prior and up until 2 hours before. Those slots get released back into the system!

**Best times to check**:
- ~24 hours before your desired time
- The night before from 8pm-12am
- The morning/day of, up until 2 hours before

**How to do it**:
1. Open the restaurant in MDE app or keep a dedicated browser tab open
2. Toggle between guest counts (3 vs 4) - this refreshes the calendar
3. Watch for green dates = availability
4. Check repeatedly throughout the day

**Pro tip**: Keep a separate browser tab dedicated to refreshing for that hard-to-get reservation, so you can still use the MDE app for Lightning Lanes and other things.

**Success rate**: 80%+ of reservations are obtained this way by experienced Disney-goers - NOT through alert software!

### Triage Booking Strategy (60-Day Window)

Book in order of difficulty, NOT by day of your trip!

**Most Difficult Reservations** (book these FIRST):
1. Ohana (Dinner)
2. Space 220 (Lounge & Restaurant)
3. California Grill 6-8pm (Peak dinner)
4. Cinderella's Royal Table
5. Yak & Yeti
6. Le Cellier (Dinner)
7. Story Book Dining at Artist Point
8. Teppan Edo (Dinner)
9. San Angel Inn
10. Sci-Fi Dine-In (difficulty fluctuates)
11. Homecomin'
12. T-Rex Cafe

### Booking Tips
- Have your desired reservations listed and sorted by difficulty BEFORE the booking window opens
- Open each restaurant in separate browser tabs (4-5 max to avoid connection issues)
- Use date range and time range filters to your advantage
- Have credit card info saved in your profile
- Move quickly - don't dilly-dally!
- Have backup dates ready

### Dining Alert Software
Options include Mouse Dining, Magical Alerts, and Thrill Data (free). These are good for booking in advance but can be seconds to minutes behind manual checking. The 24-hour manual method often works better!

## Mobile Ordering Tips

### When to Use Mobile Order
- Almost always recommended during normal meal times
- Skip it if it's very slow or off-peak times (3pm, 9pm)

### The Mobile Order Hack
- Order 1 hour BEFORE you want to eat while in line for a ride
- Food isn't made until you hit "I'm here, prepare my food"
- You can modify orders if timing changes

### If Mobile Order Shows Long Wait
If the next slot is far out (like 1+ hours), that just means mobile order slots are full. **You can still walk up to the counter and order!** There might be a short line, but you'll eat when you want.

### Mobile Order Benefits
- More modification options visible than at counter
- Easier for complicated orders / picky eaters
- Can customize sides, remove ingredients, etc.
- Kids meals have more options than stated (can get fries instead of fruit, etc.)

## Disney Springs Reservations

### Use OpenTable Instead of Disney!
Most Disney Springs restaurants have MUCH better availability on OpenTable than through the Disney website/app.

**Benefits of OpenTable**:
- More availability
- Most don't require credit card to hold
- Can cancel up until ~10 minutes before
- Easier to search and book

**How to search**: Search "Buena Vista" on OpenTable to find Disney Springs restaurants

**Exception**: Homecomin' is NOT on OpenTable - must book through Disney. However, they save lots of availability for walk-ups, so you can often get in without a reservation!

## Pro Tips

### General Dining Tips
- A Mickey pretzel or churro can be your best friend for hanger!
- Dining takes time at Disney - factor it into your touring plan
- Festival seasons = tons of extra snacking options at EPCOT
- Counter service is often faster than mobile order on slow days

### Money Saving Tips
- Split meals - Disney portions are often huge
- Snack your way through World Showcase instead of a sit-down meal
- Use mobile order to see all available modifications/sides
- Kids meals have more options than advertised (ask or check app)

### Reservation Tips
- Homecomin' saves lots of walk-up availability - don't stress about reservations there
- Tambu Lounge has same food as Ohana dinner but is much easier to get!
- OpenTable for Disney Springs = way better availability
- The 24-hour hack works for 80%+ of "hard to get" reservations

### Best Break Spots
- **Nomad Lounge** (Animal Kingdom) - Cannot recommend enough!
- **Baseline Tap House** (Hollywood Studios)
- **La Cava del Tequila** (EPCOT Mexico)
- **Space 220 Lounge** (EPCOT) - If you can get it!

## Dining Cheat Sheet

**Remember**: Dining takes time at Disney - sometimes a Mickey pretzel or churro is your best friend for hanger!

**For hard-to-get reservations**: Use the 24-hour manual refresh method - check repeatedly starting the day before, especially 8pm-midnight the night before, and morning of up to 2 hours before.

**For Disney Springs**: Use OpenTable instead of Disney's website - much better availability!

**For quick meals**: Mobile order ~1 hour before you want to eat while in line for a ride.

**Best snacking strategy**: Snack around EPCOT World Showcase during festivals!
